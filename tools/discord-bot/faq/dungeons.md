**🏰 Are dungeons in the leveling guide?**
Not yet, on purpose. The leveling guides are one solo route for Horde and one for Alliance, and getting those two right comes first. Adding dungeons would mean four routes to keep working at once. Weaving dungeons into the route is planned, and all your leveling feedback right now is what it'll be built on.

**Running dungeons today:**
**1.** Open **Dungeons** in Polestar's main menu and pick the dungeon.
**2.** Press **Pick up the quests**. Polestar builds a guide that collects its quests and their pre-quests. Open it in a **second tab** next to your leveling guide.
**3.** Run the dungeon, then press **Hand in the quests** for the turn-in trip.

Your leveling guide keeps your place in its own tab the whole time.
