- The guide menu has two resets now. Reset from here works as before and
  keeps the steps you skipped before your last quest progress. Reset
  everything clears every check-off, skip and saved step of the guide, then
  starts at the first quest you can take. In chat, `/ps reset` is Reset
  everything and `/ps resetfrom` is Reset from here. (#4111)
