- Talents: the plan's page draws a mini talent tree, the three spec columns
  side by side with their arrows, and a pick stepper above it. Step forward
  and the next pick lights up on the tree; step back and it comes off. Click a
  talent or a row in the pick list to jump to that pick; the mouse wheel over
  the tree steps too. Notes past the stepper's level are dimmed (#496).
