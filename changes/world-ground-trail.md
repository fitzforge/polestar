- An optional ground trail, in beta: sparkles on the ground ahead of you,
  along the guide's walking route. It is off until you turn it on in Settings,
  Ground trail (or `/polestar trail`). Look, at the top of that page, changes
  the whole look in one click: eight to pick from (Stardust, the default, then
  Breadcrumbs, Whisper, Ribbon, Lantern, Witcher, Fairy and Fel) and one you
  save yourself, with sliders for fine tuning. The game does not tell an addon
  how the camera is tilted, so while the trail is on Polestar sets the game's
  waypoint where the route leads and reads the angle from its marker ("Use the
  game's waypoint for the trail", on by default). That marker takes the place
  of the game's quest marker and of a map pin of your own, and quests you
  accept meanwhile are not tracked by the game. With that switch off, the
  trail is drawn short once you have moved the camera. Hiding the guide, or
  all of Polestar, hides the trail too. Where it lies on the ground is an
  estimate, and it does not know every wall (thanks DomGrieco).
