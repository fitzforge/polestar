- An optional ground trail brings the guide's route into the world as sparkles
  ahead of you. It starts off: turn it on in Options, Appearance, Ground trail
  (or `/polestar trail`) to tune its look, spacing, reach, fading and indoor
  visibility, try seven looks or save your own. Map trail
  settings stay separate. The existing first-time setup offers the switch;
  updates do not replay the welcome or add a login popup. With camera following
  set to Never, walking is not assumed to re-centre a freely turned camera.
  Short left clicks use a brief grace period instead of switching the trail off;
  longer holds still guard unmeasured camera turns. This is best-effort: a quick
  camera flick can leave the trail misaligned until camera recovery. Rendering
  pauses do not reuse an earlier click or walking-recovery interval. Camera and
  terrain placement are estimates, not exact wall occlusion (thanks DomGrieco).
