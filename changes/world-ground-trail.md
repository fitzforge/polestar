- An optional ground trail brings the guide's route into the world as sparkles
  ahead of you. It starts off: turn it on in Options, Appearance, Ground trail
  (or `/polestar trail`) to tune its look, spacing, reach, fading and indoor
  visibility, try seven looks or save your own. Map trail settings stay
  separate. Hiding the guide also hides the ground trail without changing
  its enabled setting; showing the guide restores an enabled trail. The
  existing first-time setup offers the switch; updates do not
  replay the welcome or add a login popup. With camera following set to Never,
  walking is not assumed to re-centre a freely turned camera. Short left
  clicks use a brief grace period instead of switching the trail off; longer
  holds still guard unmeasured camera turns. This is best-effort: a quick
  camera flick can leave the trail misaligned until camera recovery. Rendering
  pauses do not reuse an earlier click or walking-recovery interval. Ordinary
  right-mouse steering does not latch the trail off. Without a usable camera
  reference, first-person view omits the trail until you zoom back out.
  Accepted baked walking legs supply their pre-built height samples; when
  these are unavailable, the trail uses an ungrounded camera estimate rather
  than reading terrain for heights. Camera and ground placement, especially
  in this fallback, are approximate; turning skyward in third person can still
  misalign it. Optional sight checks are not exact wall occlusion (thanks
  DomGrieco).
