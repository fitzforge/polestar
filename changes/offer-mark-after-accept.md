- A quest you just accepted no longer reads "Not offered by <giver>". The
  window the accept itself refreshes lists the quest among your own a moment
  before the quest log does, and Polestar read that as a refusal; it now
  counts the window's own quests as taken, and an accept clears the mark, so
  abandoning the quest later no longer skips its steps (thanks thmastin,
  #127).
