  pass to the Menethil boat at about 25, not at 29; Velinde Starsong, an
  18-level carry, is no longer taken. The Wetlands guide hands in Darkshore
  quests still carried from before 0.3.5's re-order (Therylune's Escape,
  The Tower of Althalaxx, Trek to Ashenvale, Expanding Horizons) at the
  stops it already makes by their NPCs.
