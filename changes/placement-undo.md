- When Polestar places a guide for you, the guide window now says what it
  marked: "Placed you at step 14: 13 steps marked done, 3 quests skipped
  earlier", with an Undo. Undo takes back only what placement marked, never
  your own ticks and skips, and the guide stays unplaced until "Place me
  again". `/polestar placement` lists what was marked, `/polestar placement
  undo` and `/polestar placement redo` do the same from the keyboard.
