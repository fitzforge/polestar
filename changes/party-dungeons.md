- Party sync knows who runs which dungeon: "Ana: 3 steps ahead" counts only
  the steps you both have, so a friend's dungeon pick-ups never put you
  behind. When your party is at a dungeon you skip, the guide window asks
  once "Your party is running Wailing Caverns: run it with them?": Run it
  this time sets that dungeon to Run for you (your other dungeons stay as
  they are) and takes you to its door, with its quests if they are still
  ahead of you; Not now keeps your route. "Sync to my guide" onto a dungeon
  step asks the same question instead of moving you to a step that isn't
  yours, and under the dungeon's level it is not offered.
