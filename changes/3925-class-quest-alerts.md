- Class quest alerts speak up only for chains that teach a spell or ability,
  give a mount, or are on a short hand-picked list of leveling items worth the
  trip (the Whirlwind Axe, Rage of the Storm, the Celestial Orb, Verigan's
  Fist, Wolfsbane). Other item chains, such as the Enchanted Gold Bloodrobe or
  the level 60 Rhok'delar, are listed in the guide menu under your class's
  optional class quests, each naming its reward. A chain you have started
  stays in your guide. (thanks Darkfie1d, #3925)
