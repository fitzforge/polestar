- Dungeons in the leveling route: City of Dalaran. With it in your route, the
  Alliance takes the Image of Archmage Modera's Starving Arcane and Heart of
  Disruption where An Alarming Request is handed in, at about 37, runs the
  city through the western grate beside her camp and hands both in there; the
  Horde takes Melisara's The Grave Knight in Tarren Mill at about 30, runs the
  city through the sewer pipe after the Dalaran crater and hands it in on the
  next Tarren Mill stop.
