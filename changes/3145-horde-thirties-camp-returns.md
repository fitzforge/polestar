- Horde 30–40: From level 34, turn in the elder tiger hunt alongside Raptor Mastery on the way back from the Savage Coast. In Hillsbrad, Wilkes and Kalaba stay on the route if you turned in the iron shipment elsewhere.
- In Dustwallow, Krog now pays for Questioning Reethe whenever you next pass him with the quest ready. After Ratchet or Fray Island, a ready Hearthstone bound to Shadowprey Village is an option, not a required step. You can still take a flight or the road through the Charred Vale.
- After Skywatcher Plateau, characters who still need class training can visit Thunder Bluff without a quest for the city. In the Badlands, only the last half of level 38 is a coyote top-up for Indurium. Further behind, keep the flakes quest for level 39 and hand in your buzzard wings as usual.
