- Horde 30–40: From level 34, turn in the elder tiger hunt alongside Raptor Mastery on the way back from the Savage Coast. In Hillsbrad, Wilkes and Kalaba stay on the route if you turned in the iron shipment elsewhere.
- In Dustwallow, Krog now pays for a finished Reethe escort whenever you next pass him. After Ratchet or Fray Island, a ready Hearthstone bound to Shadowprey Village is an option, not a required step. You can still take a flight or the road through the Charred Vale.
