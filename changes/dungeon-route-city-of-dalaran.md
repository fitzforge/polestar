- Dungeons in the leveling route: City of Dalaran. With it in your route, the
  Alliance takes Shylamiir's A Green Sample and High Sorcerer Andromath's
  Power Overwhelming in Stormwind at about 30, runs the city from the
  Hillsbrad guide at about 32, after the Syndicate camp: An Alarming Request
  goes to the Image of Archmage Modera by the western grate, whose Starving
  Arcane and Heart of Disruption are handed in there after the run, and
  Shylamiir and Andromath take theirs on the guide's closing Stormwind stop.
  The Horde takes Melisara's The Grave Knight in Tarren Mill at about 30,
  runs the city through the sewer pipe after the Dalaran crater and hands it
  in on the next Tarren Mill stop.
