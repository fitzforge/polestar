- Alliance characters who reach Tranquil Gardens at level 30 or higher can finish Calor’s worgen hunt on the first cemetery visit. Below 30, leave the packs alone; Calor still takes a finished hunt and sends you to Jonathan Carevin.
  If you're still below 30 on the last Darkshire visit, you can keep the hunt in your log for later. Once you’re halfway through level 29, nearby Dark Runners are an option to reach 30; a group can run the Scarlet Monastery Graveyard instead.
