- New addon, Polestar: Mob Markers: every spawn of the mobs your quests need
  on the world map and minimap, one skull colour per quest and a ring per
  mob, with a legend on the world map. By default it marks only the quests
  of the guide step you're on. Quest objects you pick up or use, such as
  Dry Branches, get the guide's pickup bag instead of the skull.
