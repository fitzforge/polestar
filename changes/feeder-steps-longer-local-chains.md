- Opening a leveling guide without the guide before it hides fewer of its
  quests: a quest chain from an earlier guide that one of its quests needs
  now comes along when it takes up to about 12 minutes (5 before), as long as
  everything added to the guide stays within about 25 minutes, and only in
  the zones the guide already goes through. Those steps now always come
  before the quests they open: some came too late, and those quests read
  "Requires ... first" (Panther Mastery in Stranglethorn Vale, for one).
  Dungeon quests, group quests and quests with elites are never added this
  way (#1660, thanks creazy231).
