- Dungeons in my route: a dungeon's steps now start at its floor, the
  higher of the game's Group Finder minimum and the level players entered
  at when they went on to kill one of its bosses. Below it the guide doesn't
  ask about the dungeon, pick up its quests or send you to its door, and
  keeps you on the solo route. In the Dungeons window, "For your level" and
  the card saying a dungeon is open to you start from the same level.
