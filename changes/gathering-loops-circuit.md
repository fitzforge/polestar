- Profession guides: a farming loop is now one circuit. The map and minimap
  draw the loop you are on as a closed track with a small mark on each node
  spot, and the arrow follows it along the line from point to point, lap after
  lap, with no stops: mining or picking never holds it, and it never jumps
  about as you walk. Step off the track and it leads you back to the nearest
  point ahead. With TomTom's arrow or the travel planner off, it points at the
  next loop point. A zone change no longer swaps the loop you are on for
  another one.
