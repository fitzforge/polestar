- In Stranglethorn, take Kebok’s tusk quest upstairs, track necklaces with the first Skullsplitters, and keep the fangs from the Mistvale elders you already hunt. Do your held Wild Shore errands before entering Booty Bay and hand in what is ready.
- At Grom'gol, hand in the necklaces with the reef discovery and the basilisk shards with Mok’thardin’s water. If only one is ready, hand it in without waiting for the other.
- In Feralas, bind before visiting the northern huts in Camp Mojache. Use Orwin's shovel at a fresh patch of dirt and loot the cobalt that appears.
- In Tanaris, look for Tooga after finishing the dew-gland hunt. Escort him to Torta if he is there; otherwise skip just his pickup and carry on. Yeh'kinya still takes any finished spirits at the port. Gadgetzan's last stop reminds you to set aside 15 Silk Cloth before leaving for the gorge.
