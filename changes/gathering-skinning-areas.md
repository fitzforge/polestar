- Skinning: every skill range offers areas in more zones, for every race, from
  the starting areas on: Durotar's Razor Hill and Razormane Grounds, Mulgore's
  Thunderhorn Water Well and Golden Plains, Tirisfal's Brill and Brightwater
  Lake, Elwynn's Tower of Azora and Jerod's Landing, Darkshore, Silverpine,
  the Barrens and on. The beast lists gained the Barrens giraffes and hyenas,
  Darkshore's striders, Duskwood's worgen, Ashenvale's bucks, the Dustwallow
  and Tanaris turtles, hyenas and basilisks, and other creatures the game lets
  you skin. The leather the crafting guides send you for is skinned only from
  creatures that can be skinned, never from another creature that shares a
  skinnable one's name (thanks willsarg).
