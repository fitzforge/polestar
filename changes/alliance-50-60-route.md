- Western Plaguelands (Alliance, 50-60): Janice Felstone's Better Late Than
  Never moves from the Burning Steppes guide's Chillwind stop at about 51 to the
  Winterspring guide's Felstone Field cauldron stop at about 55: the ghouls
  around her farmhouse are level 54. Davil's Libram waits for the Silithus
  guide's Hearthglen visit at about 60, right after the overlook, and the step
  warns that the town hall's Scarlet Paladins and priests are elites, with a
  level 63 High Clerist beside the libram; the Western Plaguelands guide no
  longer sends you into Hearthglen at 56. Winterspring's Strange Sources no
  longer tells you to take the elites on the way one at a time: it keeps you
  on the road past the Frostmaul giants, touches the mouth of Darkwhisper Gorge
  and turns back before the Hederine elites.
