- More group quests in the leveling guides: the elite quests a party of
  three gains the most from, 32 more for the Horde and 12 more for the
  Alliance (The Real Threat and Call to Arms in Arathi, Vile Priestess Hexx
  in the Hinterlands, Kum'isha's errands in the Blasted Lands and more). The
  guide asks at each whether to do it with a group or skip it, as it now does
  for Shinyfinder Narf and Nimsy in Elwynn Forest, Vulgara on Zephras Isle,
  Bruuz in The Barrens and Aggor in Durotar; skipped, the route is the solo
  one as before.
