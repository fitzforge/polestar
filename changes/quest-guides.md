- Quests: see which Polestar guides use a quest. Its tooltip in the quest log
  and the tracker, its details and a quest giver's list name each guide for
  your character that picks it up, works it or hands it in, with the guide's
  levels; a small guide mark after the title in the quest log, the tracker
  and quest givers' lists says one does (in the accent when an open guide
  does). So a quest for a later guide, or a dungeon pick-up guide's
  pre-quest, can be taken early or kept, and one no guide uses dropped.
  Quality of Life > Quests > Guides for each quest (thanks Liquidor).
