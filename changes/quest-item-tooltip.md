- Polestar's quest lines on mob, object, quest giver and item tooltips put
  the quest's level before its name and colour the name by difficulty (grey,
  green, yellow, orange, red). An item a quest in your log handed you says
  "In your quest log" or "Turn in" beside that quest, and a leftover quest
  item names the quests you handed in for it. With Questie, the new setting
  Replace Questie's quest lines (under Quest tooltips) shows each quest once:
  Questie leaves out its lines for the quests Polestar names. (thanks Olivia)
- Leftover quest items (#4272): the red bag border now also marks a quest
  item whose quest is grey for you, one you abandoned or never took (unless
  the item begins it), or one not for your character, as long as no guide
  ahead needs it and you haven't marked it to keep. Its tooltip names each
  quest with why: Done, Grey, Not in your log, or Not for you. An item for a
  grey quest still in your log says to abandon the quest first. When your
  bags run low, the bag-space button offers to delete leftovers no quest in
  your log asks for (two presses, as for junk), never a quest starter.
  (thanks Qabhal-Oğuz, Bruatel, gidi_)
