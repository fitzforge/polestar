- The party leader can share their route choices: their answers at group
  quests and the dungeons turned on for leveling guides. Members who run
  Polestar get a note in the guide window, "Ana leads the party. Group
  quests: 2 with the group, 1 skipped. Take the same route?", or take it at
  once, or ignore it. Turn sharing on, and pick Ask me, Take it or Ignore it,
  in Options, Questing, Quests, Party.
