- Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid and Cooking guides, and Mining + Engineering:
  rebuilt as single steps a first-timer can follow, each with the arrow on its
  place. Enchanting starts by disenchanting the green items you carry (each
  gives a point early on), takes its dust from disenchanting or the Auction
  House, and sends you to Annora inside Uldaman for Artisan. First Aid and
  Cooking buy their rank books and manuals in steps of their own, and learn
  the trainer's recipe before the trip to the book's seller. Cooking's fire
  step also finishes when you place a Basic Campfire Kit.
- Fishing + Cooking: new waters and recipes from Fishing 75 on (Longjaw Mud
  Snapper in the capitals to 130, catfish water to 205, trout water to 255,
  Feralas to 300 for Redgill and Nightfin Soup), and no more steps that were
  already done when they appeared.
- A recipe learned in an earlier stretch is learned again where a later one
  needs it, for a player who starts the guide past that stretch.
