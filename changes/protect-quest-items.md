- Items a quest still needs get a yellow "?" in your bags, and their tooltip
  names the quest, until you hand it in. That covers quests in your log and
  quests your guide has ahead that you have not accepted yet. Deleting such
  an item adds "Needed for: <quest>" to the game's confirmation. Sell one at
  a vendor and Polestar asks whether to buy it back, and its own automatic
  sale of grey items keeps it (thanks jaff). The marks and the buyback
  question have their own settings under Questing.
