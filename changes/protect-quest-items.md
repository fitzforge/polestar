- Items a quest still needs get a yellow "?" in your bags, and their tooltip
  names the quest, until you hand it in. That covers quests in your log and
  quests your guide has ahead that you have not accepted yet. Deleting such
  an item adds "Needed for: <quest>" to the game's confirmation, and the
  automatic sale of grey items at a vendor keeps it (thanks jaff). The mark
  and the delete line have their own settings under Questing.
