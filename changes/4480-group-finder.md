- Group Finder (off by default): find the players on your quests who run
  Polestar and group with them in a click. Turn it on in Settings > Group
  Finder; it then opens from the guide window's menu, from a button on each
  open kill, collect or complete goal, and from the quest menus. A search
  only goes out when you ask for it; hello, goodbye, announce and invite
  lines only on your click or key, each one editable.
