- When the route casts a class teleport (a Mage's city teleports, a Druid's
  Teleport: Moonglade, a Shaman's Astral Recall) or uses the Hearthstone, the
  Journey shows that spell's or the Hearthstone's own icon, and the Hearthstone
  button offers the cast: one click (or the step key) casts it. The button
  stays hidden on a flight, without the spell or its Rune of Teleportation, and
  a guide's own Hearth step keeps it for the Hearthstone.
- When the route waits for your Hearthstone (or Astral Recall) to come off
  cooldown because that is still the fastest way, the Journey and the step say
  "Wait 4:12 for your Hearthstone, then hearth to Razor Hill", counting down,
  and the arrow says "Hearthstone ready in 4:12" until it is ready.
