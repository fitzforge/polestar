- Riverglades (Horde): the route checks your level before the Eastsea camps.
  Below 44, finish Argo's mushroom and sea monster quests before facing the
  raiders and Admiral Lamora. Coming from Kargath early? The guide now points
  you east to Rog'mar, away from the elite patrols below the pass.
- Argo now takes a sea monster's head you already have before the pirate camps. On either Horde visit to Booty Bay, Dudd takes the Partial Mystical Notes if you have them ready.
