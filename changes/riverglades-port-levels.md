- Riverglades (Horde): the route checks your level before the Eastsea camps.
  At 42 you can work the first camp; the raiders and Admiral Lamora wait for
  44. Argo takes a ready hand-in on the same dock visit without sending you
  inland and back. Coming from Kargath early? Follow the road east to Rog'mar,
  away from the elite patrols below the pass.
- On either Horde visit to Booty Bay, Dudd takes the Partial Mystical Notes if you have them ready.
