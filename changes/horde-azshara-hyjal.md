- Horde: Take the lower Azshara quests before Felwood, and hunt in Un'Goro before the Silithus top-ups. The farmhouse keys wait for the later visit to Dalson's Tears.
- Both factions can choose Mount Hyjal. Bring help for the elite-lined gorge; solo players can stay on the Silithus route. Horde players visit Elderwild and return through Everlook.
- In Mount Hyjal, check Bluebell's flight map before leaving for Moonglade. If she cannot take you, the guide waits for safe transport rather than pointing through the gorge.
