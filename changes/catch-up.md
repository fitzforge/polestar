- Open a guide on a character who already did part of the route and it catches
  up: the guide window goes over every step already done, faster and faster,
  while the arrow turns an hourglass over, and stops where you are. Grey
  quests are now left out by default (Options, "Skip grey quests"), and so are
  green ones behind your progress that you have not taken, unless a later
  quest needs them. A guide you are past hands over to the next one, without
  the out-levelled banner; the first time, the guides go by from your race's
  first one. Item, mount, reputation, profession and class guides do every
  step, and "Do every step" in the guide window's menu does the same for a
  leveling guide.
