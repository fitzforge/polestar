- The WoW Forever, Blizzard and Classic skins each draw their own waypoint
  arrow and map pins in the game's art, in place of Polestar's gold dart and
  medallions, at the same size: WoW Forever its quest arrow and the campaign
  quest's shield, Blizzard the world map's arrow and the quest marker,
  Classic the vanilla world map's arrow and the minimap's dark disc. The
  arrow keeps its own colours until you are close, then takes the arrival
  colour as before; an arrow colour you picked still applies.
