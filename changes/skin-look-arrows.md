- The WoW Forever, Blizzard and Classic skins each draw their own waypoint
  arrow and map pins in the game's art, in place of Polestar's gold dart and
  medallions: WoW Forever its quest arrow on the campaign quest's shield,
  Blizzard the game's navigation arrow on its quest marker, Classic the
  vanilla minimap arrow on the minimap's dark disc. The arrow keeps its own
  colours until you are close, then takes the arrival colour as before; an
  arrow colour you picked still applies.
