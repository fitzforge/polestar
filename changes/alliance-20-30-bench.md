- Alliance route 20-30 now goes Ashenvale and Stonetalon, the first Wetlands
  visit, then Duskwood and Redridge together: Duskwood's first loop,
  Redridge's first pass, Duskwood's second loop, Redridge's second pass. You
  reach Redridge at its level, so the grind to 23 at the Blackrock camps is
  gone (thanks to the player who reported on Discord that Redridge left you
  two levels short of Duskwood). Redridge's second pass at about 26 brings
  Tharil'zun, Shadow Magic, Gath'Ilzogg, Incinerator Gar'im and Lieutenant
  Fangore, with a tip to group up for the elites, and Duskwood asks for level
  23 before Worgen in the Woods.
- After the second Wetlands visit the boat takes you straight on to
  Ashenvale's 27-32 quests (they used to wait until 33-34), Making Do goes to
  Thundris on the way, and the grind to 28 before Pilot Longbeard is gone.
- The Thandol Span quests are done on the Dun Modr visit before the Southshore
  boat, where the route already crosses the span, instead of a walk back onto
  the span from Arathi at 37. This shortens the top-up there if you skip
  Nek'rosh. Lightforge Ingots is no longer offered at 27, since it needs The
  Lost Ingots first.
