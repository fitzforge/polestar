section: players
---
- Turning dungeons on is easy to follow. The guide window's menu has
  Dungeons on your way: Skip them, Ask me or Run them, and the next dungeons
  on your route with about the level each comes at and how many of its
  quests are picked up where, each with its own switch. A dungeon you run
  takes every one of its quests; where some are off the way, "Only quests on
  my way" leaves those out and says how much time that saves.
- Whatever you change, there or in the settings, the guide window says at
  once what it means ("Dungeons on: next is Wailing Caverns, at about level
  21. Its 7 quests are picked up on your way through The Barrens and Thunder
  Bluff.", or when and where the next one comes if it's further off), and
  turning dungeons off says what happens to the dungeon quests already in
  your log.
- A few steps before a run, Find a group opens the game's Group Finder (you
  pick the dungeon there). At the door you answer I'm in a group or Skip this
  one; in a party the run is simply on, with Skip this one still there. After
  Skip this one the guide says which quests stay in your log: finished ones
  are still handed in, and Clean up quest log takes the others. The group
  finder line now shows only on the run itself.
- The dungeon card offers Add to my route for a dungeon your leveling route
  can still take.
