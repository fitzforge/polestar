- Class macros: a new Macros section in the hub (`/pole macros`) lists
  ready-made macros for your class, by spec and PvE or PvP, with what each
  does, its modifier keys and what it needs, and the spell names in your
  game's language. One click puts a macro on your character's or your
  account's macro list, out of combat; a card offers Update when its text
  changes in a later release. The macros come from Classic WoW Forever's
  macro pages (CC BY 4.0), marked as written for Classic, adapted or new in
  Forever. Turn them off under Options > Discovery > Lookup.
