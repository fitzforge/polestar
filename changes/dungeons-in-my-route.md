- Dungeons in my route: Skip them, Ask me, or Run them, for each character, on
  the welcome's "Make it yours" page and in Options, Guide, Automation. A new
  character is asked; one you already play keeps to its solo route until you
  change it. With Ask me, the guide stops at a dungeon's first quest on your
  way and asks: Run it or Skip it, remembered for that dungeon (Options,
  Discovery, Dungeons, changes one). A few steps before the run the guide
  window says to start looking for a group, and at the door "No group? Skip
  it" drops the dungeon and goes on with the solo route: its quests stay in
  your log and nothing waits on them. Changing your mind mid-guide never sends
  you back for a pick-up you passed.
