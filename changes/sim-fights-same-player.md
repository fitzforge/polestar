section: forge
---
- `forge sim fights` measures the mob-level and elite terms inside one
  player's kills at one level, so a strong player who goes for harder mobs no
  longer makes those mobs look fast; level and class are still fitted across
  players. The report says how many groups of kills and elite cells that
  rests on (`groups`, `eliteCells`), how many cells the level and class fit
  kept (`cellsUsed`), and what the three terms read with every player set
  against every other (`allPlayers`); schema 2 (#2884, thanks
  MartinPTielemans).
