- The Alliance Riverglades guide drops the quests you carry for the guides it
  takes the place of (Desolace's south loop at the start, and from level 43
  the Stranglethorn Vale and Badlands quests), so taking the Riverglades,
  Shen'dralas and Mount Hyjal one after another never fills your quest log
  past the game's 40. The experimental guides also warn of the elites near
  each camp: Chogg'Zac in Sunnyglade, the Twilight Enforcers by the ogre mine,
  Ghostmaw along the river, Rog'mar's guards by the training grounds, the
  Valley of Bones by Shen'dralas' western pylon, and Mount Hyjal's elites.
