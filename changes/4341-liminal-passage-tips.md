- Mage: on a long trip, while you rest in an inn or a city, a card tells you
  when a Scroll of Liminal Passage saves time. It names the party member who
  rests at an inn and the time the scroll saves. If you carry no scroll, it
  tells you to buy some at the auction house and points the arrow to the
  auctioneer. The card only tells you: you use the scroll yourself. Turn it
  off under Settings > Guide > Arrow & routes > Routes, "Liminal Passage
  tips" (thanks cryptiklemur, #4341).
