- The target button becomes a list of the step's mobs (or, on a step with no
  mob, the NPCs it sends you to), each with its portrait, what it drops for
  the step and the goal's count (3/8): click a name to target that one. The
  crosshair behind a portrait turns green, and the row a faint green, while
  one is close enough to target. A small Polestar mark beside the list moves
  the buttons; a left-click on it folds the list to one row showing the mob
  that is close, and a right-click opens the settings; the target button's
  key binding targets the first mob in the list that is close (in Quick
  Keybind Mode, hover the mark to bind it). Options > Questing > Buttons sets
  how many names it shows (1-8). To keep the single button that moves to the
  next name with each click, as before, turn on Simple target button there.
  (thanks petllama, #1181)
