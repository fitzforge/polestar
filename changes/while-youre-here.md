- While you're here: when you pass through the area of a later step's
  objective whose quest is already in your log, a line slides in under the
  current step, for example "While you're here: Kill Kobold Vermin, 12 more
  (step 14) 45 yd". Any later step of the guide counts, also one after the
  guide has taken you to another zone and back, and it goes by where you
  are, not where the arrow points: chasing slow drops past another step's
  objective catches it. With two close by, the one you can only do around
  here (a pool, a camp) comes before mobs found all over the zone. A mob it
  needs that you can see (its nameplate, your target) counts too, wherever
  its usual spawns are. It stays while you work on it. Grab it now and
  that step is done or shorter when you get there. Click the line to point
  the arrow there; right-click it (or its close button) and it stays away for
  the rest of the zone. Left alone for three minutes it goes, and comes back
  when you meet more of its mobs. A soft chime plays and its mark on the minimap
  pulses so you know to look (at the minimap's edge when it is further
  out), and the quest item button offers its item when the step has none.
  A quest ready to hand in to someone standing right there shows the same
  way. With the guide window hidden, one small toast says it. Never in
  dungeons, on flights or boats, or during timed and escort quests.
  Options, Guide, Window, "While you're here" turns it off, sets how close
  (60 to 250 yards, 150 by default), the hand-ins and the sound. A bug
  report says what the line shows, or why none does.
