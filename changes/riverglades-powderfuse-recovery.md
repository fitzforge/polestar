- Horde Riverglades: almost 42 or 44 at Powderfuse Port? The guide points out a nearby beach or pirate camp for the last few kills. If you're further behind, you can skip the camp without losing ready hand-ins. Leaving without Chogg'Zac now points you to Grakna before the flight to Kargath.
- Finished Chogg'Zac with a party but heading west alone? Below 43, the guide leaves Da'grosh's camps for later and lists earned rewards on the way out. At 43 you can do the ordinary cultists, scouts and miners solo. Kilgar remains a group choice.
