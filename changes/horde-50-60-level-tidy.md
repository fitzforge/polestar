- Horde 50-60: quests whose enemies stood two or more levels above you come on
  the later visits that fit them: the Un'Goro pterrordax and tar pits on the
  Silithus guide's crater loop, Terrordale and Tirion's hunts in the Eastern
  Plaguelands, Kirsta's Scarlets, Mulgris' grizzlies and Nathanos' northern
  hunts on the Silithus guide's Plaguelands ride, the yeti horns and
  Winterfall Village later in Winterspring. Short top-ups, only when you are
  under the level, come before the camps that need them, on mobs a quest wants
  where there are some. The Searing Gorge moves to the Un'Goro guide, and
  Stealing Knowledge with its deliveries to the Azshara guide. The Rise of the
  Machines (War Reavers up to 55 at about 53) is left out.
