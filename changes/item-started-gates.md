section: players
---
- Quests started from an item you picked up earlier now show only while you carry
  the item, so a skipped, lost or sold item no longer holds the guide where nothing
  can start the quest: the three OOX distress beacons in the Mechanical Chicken
  guide and the Nibbled-On Book in the Stormwind reputation guide.
- Companion pet guides (Mechanical Chicken, Smolderweb Carrier, Worg Carrier,
  Spectral Bear Cub) no longer wait for a pet you already earned: the hand-in step
  shows only while its quest is still to hand in.
