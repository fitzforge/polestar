- Tooltips: an uncommon (green) or rare (blue) weapon or armour tells a
  character with Enchanting what disenchanting it gives, "Disenchants into:
  1–2 Strange Dust (or 1–2 Lesser Magic Essence)": the likeliest result with
  its count, and the next one when it comes up often. Armour gives mostly
  dust, weapons mostly essence, rare items a shard. Characters without
  Enchanting see no line, nor do items that can't be disenchanted.
