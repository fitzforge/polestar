- Dungeons in the leveling route (Horde): a dungeon you run now brings every
  quest of it you can do before 60, with the quests that open them, each
  picked up and handed in where the route passes or on a short trip from it:
  Thrall's Hidden Enemies, Varimathras and Rahauro for Ragefire Chasm; Nara
  Wildmane's Leaders of the Fang, Serpentbloom and The Glowing Shard for the
  Wailing Caverns; Rear Guard Patrol, The Wrath of Rath'mael and Unending
  Torment for the Ruins of Lordaeron; The Book of Ur for Shadowfang Keep;
  Blackfathom Villainy; Source of Power and the Dalaran chain to Heart of
  Disruption; Blueleaf Tubers and A Vengeful Fate for Razorfen Kraul; The
  Prophecy of Mosh'aru, Tiara of the Deep and Gahz'rilla for Zul'Farrak;
  Disharmony of Flame, Lost Thunderbrew Recipe, Ribbly Screwspigot and The
  Heart of the Mountain for Blackrock Depths; Jammal'an the Prophet and The
  Essence of Eranikus for the Sunken Temple; and the class quests a run
  finishes (The Orb of Soran'ruk, Brutal Armor, Elemental Aid). Quests a run
  leaves unfinished are abandoned at their last hand-in, so nothing stays in
  your log. Choose "Only quests on my way" for a dungeon to keep just the
  quests the route passes.
