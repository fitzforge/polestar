- Dungeon guides: a dungeon's pick-up guide now shows the dungeon itself: its picture and name, then the step's own quests and the way in with its zone and travel time, a missing key and Guide me to the entrance once nothing is left before the door. It repeats nothing the Dungeons page and the step list already show: no level range, group or list of the run's quests, and only a character under the dungeon's floor gets one line more, "Level 15: come back at 18".
- Dungeon tracker: inside a dungeon or raid where you hold its quests, a small tracker lists what is left for them with live counts, the bosses in kill order checked off as they die, and "Loot <item> from <boss>" when a boss drops something your quest needs. It shows even while the guide window hides in dungeons, docks into the guide window when you open it there, and puts a quest's item on the quest item button. Options > Trackers > Dungeon tracker.
- Dungeon guides: each step shows only the quests of its stop, as the leveling view does; the step list (the numbered button at the top) holds every pick-up step. Quest givers who stand together (the same spot, or within 60 yards) are one step.
- Dungeon guides: the guide window's general row of finished quests ("4 quests ready to hand in") is not shown on a dungeon's pick-up or hand-in guide: its quests are the run's own, and the Quest room lists every finished quest in your log.
