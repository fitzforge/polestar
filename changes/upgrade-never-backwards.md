- Updating Polestar in the middle of a guide no longer sends you back. The
  new quests an update puts behind where you are are passed for you, and so
  are new travel steps there. Your check-offs, skips and the step you were
  looking at stay on the same goals even when their step changed. A quest you
  are carrying keeps its steps. A quest ahead that needs one of the passed
  quests gets it back just before it if it's on your way. Otherwise that quest
  is left out of your guide.
