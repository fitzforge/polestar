- Polestar Experimental: Shen'dralas, Forever's new zone south of Desolace,
  for both factions at levels 42-44. It flies you to Desolace and through the
  Valley of Bones, then follows Mordent Evenshade's pylons (with a trip back
  to Desolace's two centaur villages), the Tattered Journal, Magister
  Kirandis and Mithera's Forlorn Gardens, with Historian Biraxas's heart as
  an extra. The quest log now lists all three lines of Strange Pylons, one
  per pylon.
