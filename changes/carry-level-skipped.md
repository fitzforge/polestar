- A quest you were a level too low for when the guide passed it is no longer
  dropped when the guide ends first. A later guide picks it up where its route
  passes the quest giver, and comes back for it once you reach the level; the
  skip message now says what will happen to it. Steps that only show while you
  carry a quest or from a level now come back later in the same guide once that
  holds, instead of staying skipped.
