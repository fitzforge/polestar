- While you're here: with "Click objectives to target" on, clicking a line
  targets its mob or NPC and marks it like a step's objective, as well as
  pointing the arrow there. A clicked pick-up or hand-in line also puts its
  NPC first on the target button (thanks imp). With click-to-target on,
  middle-clicking an objective row opens its quest in the quest log again.
