- While you're here: with "Click objectives to target" on, clicking a line
  targets its mob or NPC and marks it like a step's objective, as well as
  pointing the arrow there. The target button puts the clicked pick-up or
  hand-in NPC first. With click-to-target on, middle-clicking an objective
  row opens its quest in the quest log again (thanks imp and CDVolvik, #3281).
