- Browse all steps: a click on the step number in the guide window lists
  every step of the guide in place of the step card, each with its people
  and goals, done ones ticked and the live step marked. A click on a step
  looks at it; a second click on the number or Escape closes the list. In
  every look and in compact mode (thanks TTik3r, #1678).
