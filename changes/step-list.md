- The guide window's step now sits in its own, much denser card: smaller text
  with a glyph on every goal, the count, the quest's level and the distance to
  the arrow's target at the right, mobs and NPCs in the NPC colour, and the
  "Then:" lines for the people after the one in focus as one small, muted line
  each. The
  Journey shows a summary (the first step and a faint line for the rest) until
  you click it. The step's notes and its optional goals, with their spots and a Skip button
  (and the gold bar when the arrow leads to one), now stand in two cards above
  the window instead of inside the step card; the tip stays at the bottom of
  the card and no longer spills over its edge. The class quest's card and the
  "Looking ahead" notice are one tight row each. A new list button in the navigator, in compact mode too, shows
  every step of the guide as a scrolling list of cards: each names who to talk
  to and every goal, with their counts and ticks, and a click looks at that
  step. Compact mode is two lines of card without the progress bar. Only the
  default Polestar look changes.
- When the guide window's goals scroll under its height cap, the view no longer
  cuts a wrapped "Then:" line in half.
