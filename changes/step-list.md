- A new guide window. The step sits in one dense card that says each thing
  once: a glyph, the count, the quest's level and the distance on every goal
  row, and the zone on the title row.
- The step's notes, "While you're here" and its optional goals stand in small
  cards above the window instead of inside it.
- Browse all steps: a click on the step number lists every step of the guide;
  a click on one looks at it.
- The tip is a one-line strip at the foot of the card, in the full window and
  in compact. Compact mode is one surface of about half the height.
- Quieter chrome: one-line notices, the bug report in the ⋯ menu, and the class
  quest and dungeon heads-up slides out over the cards instead of behind them.
- The step list and the cards above the window work in every look; the dense
  card is the default Polestar look's.
- When the guide window's goals scroll under its height cap, the view no
  longer cuts a wrapped "Then:" line in half.
