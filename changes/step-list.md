- The guide window's step now sits in its own, much denser card: smaller text
  with a glyph on every goal, the count, the quest's level and the distance to
  the arrow's target at the right, mobs and NPCs in the NPC colour, and the
  "Then:" lines for the people after the one in focus as one small, muted line
  each. The
  Journey shows a summary (the first step and a faint line for the rest) until
  you click it. The step's notes and its optional goals, with their count and a Skip button
  (and the gold bar when the arrow leads to one), now stand in two cards above
  the window instead of inside the step card; the tip stays at the bottom of
  the card and no longer spills over its edge. "While you're here" is a card of the same stack, with the step it is for.
  The Optional card shows how many to kill or collect. The class quest's card and the
  "Looking ahead" notice are one tight row each. The step number in the navigator opens the step list (there is no
  list button); it opens on the step you are looking at, with the earlier ones
  above; finished steps are one line and every card carries its title.
  The list shows every step of the guide as a scrolling list of cards: each names who to talk
  to and every goal, with their counts and ticks, and a click looks at that
  step. The step's title row holds its zone at the right and its goals sit one step in; the XP row and the progress bar are one tight pair. Compact mode is two lines of card without the progress bar. The dense card is
  the default Polestar look's; the step list and the cards above the window are
  in every look.
- When the guide window's goals scroll under its height cap, the view no longer
  cuts a wrapped "Then:" line in half.
