- Junk and keep marks have two key bindings, Mark as junk and Mark to keep,
  in Polestar's section of the game's Key Bindings and under the marks'
  switch in Quality of Life, Vendors (unbound until you set them). Each acts
  on the item under the mouse: Mark as junk marks it as junk or takes the
  junk mark off, Mark to keep does the same for keep, and each swaps the
  other mark for its own. A new switch, Ctrl+Alt-click cycles (on), turns the
  click off for players who only use the keys (#2125).
