- Steps where an item has to be used now say so with a row of their own, and
  the quest item button shows that item when it is in your bags. Tanaris Field
  Sampling has a row to loot each untested sample, a row to use it and then the
  count of acceptable samples, so the button shows the sample to use.
  Twenty-nine more quests whose item use or click lived only in the step's
  note get the same rows,
  among them Egg Hunt (the Digging Claw), A Little Slime Goes a Long Way, Dark
  Heart, A Plague Upon Thee, Arcane Runes, To Kill With Purpose, The Red
  Crystal, Plagued Lands, Elemental Bracers, Testing the Wells, The Angry
  Scytheclaws, Dwarven Digging, New Life and Trial of the Sea Lion (thanks to
  the player who reported Tanaris Field Sampling on CurseForge).
- The quest item button no longer offers an item the quest only asks you to
  carry back, such as Tanaris Field Sampling's Field Testing Kit, or a quest
  log item that cannot be used.
