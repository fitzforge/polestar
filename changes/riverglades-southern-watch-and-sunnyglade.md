- Alliance Riverglades: at level 41, visit Gelinda on the way out of the Bristle Hills and pay Dalton when you return to Southern Watch. Below 41 you can collect Dalton's reward first and meet Gelinda later. In Sunnyglade, talk to the farmer on the way into town, not on a second trip out.
- Below 42, collect finished shore quests at Powderfuse Port before the first pirate camp. Its musketeers can be level 43. Skipping the pirate top-up below 44 no longer sends you up the cliffs to fight the level-45 raiders or Admiral Lamora. Finished quests can still be handed in at the port.
