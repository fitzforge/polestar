- Stats: a new page in the hub (/pole stats, the minimap button's menu and the
  guide window's menu) that counts what your character does: quests,
  experience, time played, gold, deaths and where you play, with a map of the
  paths you walked, drawn on one map of the world that shows each zone in
  more detail as you zoom in, and of your journeys: the flight paths you
  flew, the boats and zeppelins you rode, and your portals, hearths and tram
  rides. The map's Quests and Rares tabs show the share of each zone's
  quests you have done and of its rares you have killed, and a Completion
  page lists them zone by zone. Kept on your computer only. It is an addon of its own,
  Polestar Stats: Options > Discovery > Stats turns it off (it is then not
  loaded at all) or only parts of it (time played, experience, quests, kills
  and deaths, gold, the heat map, the walked path, journeys, professions and
  dungeons), and a player who does not want it can leave the Polestar_Stats
  folder out.
