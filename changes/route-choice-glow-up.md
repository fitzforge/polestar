- Choose your path has a new look. Each card opens on a larger painting of
  the zone with the route's start marked on it, shows the levels, the trip
  there and the quests as icons, and says where the route goes next ("Then:
  Feralas & Tanaris"). Polestar's experimental guides wear an Experimental
  chip and a "Try" button, and a zone whose quests are mostly new in Forever
  carries the ∞ pennant ("Mark quests new in Forever" turns it off with the
  mark). When the routes go on to different guides, the window no longer
  says they meet again.
