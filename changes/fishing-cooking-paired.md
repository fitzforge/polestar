- Fishing + Cooking is built like the other paired guides: each cooking stretch
  starts with a fishing step for the fish it still needs ("Fish 20 Raw Bristle
  Whisker Catfish, Fishing 117 to 150"), whose farm spot is the one nearby water
  that gets you them soonest, walked stand by stand. Waters by the other
  faction's guards, by elites or by creatures above your level are left out,
  and a water where players often died counts as slower. In a paired guide the
  materials it gathers itself are gathered by default; Mix still lets you buy
  them.
