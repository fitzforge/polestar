- Free mode: a new order, Balanced, now the default. It weighs the XP a quest
  pays against the walk and the work left, and goes first where several of your
  quests share the place or the mobs, to a quest you've already started, and to
  an NPC who takes two or more of your finished quests; it stays with your
  quest while you walk instead of jumping to whichever is a few yards nearer.
  The Free mode card says why the arrow chose its quest ("3 of your quests
  here · started · hand-in on the way"). Nearest first and Most XP a minute
  first are still in Settings, the card and `/polestar free nearest` or `xp`;
  `/polestar free balanced` goes back. The work a quest takes is now counted
  from how long kills and drops take players at your level.
