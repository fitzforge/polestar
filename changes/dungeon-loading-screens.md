- The Dungeons page's Map tab now opens on the dungeon's own loading screen, the
  picture the game shows on the way in, read from your game client and cropped
  to fit. A button on the picture's corner (A on a gamepad) swaps it for the
  map with the entrance and the quest givers, and back; Polestar remembers the
  choice until you reload. A dungeon on a continent's own loading screen, such
  as the Searing Basin or Caverns of Time, keeps the map.
