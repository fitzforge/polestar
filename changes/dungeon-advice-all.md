- "Which dungeons" next to "Suggest dungeons" (Options, Discovery, Dungeons):
  Only those that pay (the default) or Every dungeon in range. With every
  dungeon, each dungeon you reach the levels of is a fork in the road in the
  guide window: pick up its quests for a guided route to it, or keep
  levelling and come back later.
