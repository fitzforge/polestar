- Guide window: the menu is shorter and in groups. Compact mode,
  Transparent, Lock window and Pause are a row of icons at its top, lit
  while on; pointing at one names it and says On or Off in a tooltip. While
  Polestar is paused, a line under the row says "Polestar is paused" and
  shows the name and state there instead. Hide window leaves the menu: hidden
  from there, the window had no way back but `/polestar show`. Then come the guide and
  its tabs, the step, the quest log, settings and tools, each group under a
  thin line, and Reset guide progress alone at the foot, in red (it still
  asks twice). Every entry is in view, never a list to filter. Skip this
  step, Report a problem and Lookup leave the menu while the header shows
  their buttons, and come back when those are off. The pin rows have their
  pin icon. In the quest log group, "Hand in finished quests" and "Clean up
  quest log" are always there: greyed out as "None to hand in" and "Quest
  log is clean" when there is nothing to do.
