- Leveling guides no longer have steps that only send you somewhere ("Into
  Eastern Plaguelands", "Fly to Ironforge", "Boat to Menethil Harbor"): the
  next step with something to do leads straight there and the arrow's route
  picks the way, a class teleport, the Hearthstone, a flight or a boat
  included. The old checkpoint is kept as a hint the arrow goes by only while
  it is on your way; once you are past it, came another way or have a faster
  way, it is dropped, so a Mage who teleports is never pulled back to it. The
  step says what and where ("Pamela Redpath · Eastern Plaguelands") with the
  route's way under it. Doors and cave mouths the walking maps can't see, and
  Sanath Lim-yo's teleport, stay steps of their own, and pass once the step
  after them is done. (thanks uNBEx)
