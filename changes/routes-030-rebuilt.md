- The leveling routes are rebuilt from the game's data: each race's own
  starting zone, then one Horde route from the Barrens and one Alliance route
  from Westfall to 60, the same for every race of a faction. Before shipping,
  the guides are played through the way you follow them, and a step that
  can't be done as written is refused. Against 0.2.6, played the same way:
  no hearth while the stone is cooling down (there were 3 to 8 per
  character), the orders players reported as wrong are followed, nearly
  every quest that needs a rare drop is an optional extra, about a quarter
  fewer open
  quests are walked past, and 2.5 to 3.5 hours of grinding became questing.
  The hours to 60 stay about the same.
