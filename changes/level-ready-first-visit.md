- Ashenvale (Alliance, 23-25) and Desolace (Horde, 33-35): quests the route
  saves for a later visit because their mobs are above most players there now
  come on the first visit when you're already at their level. Raene
  Wolfrunner's Foulweald and Dal Bloodclaw hunts are offered from 23.5 and
  done from 24; Drulzegar's Other Fish to Fry is offered from 33.5 and done
  from 34, in the southern half of Sar'theris Strand. Below those levels the
  route is unchanged (thanks Twerkjob and Crest).
