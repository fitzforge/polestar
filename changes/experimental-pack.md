- Polestar Experimental comes with Polestar: guides for Forever's new zones
  while they are still being written and tested (alpha), starting with
  Riverglades. It stays off, and nothing of it loads, until you switch it on
  under Options > Community guides; its guides then come into the guide menu
  under Community guides at once, and it loads at every login while it stays
  on. Polestar never suggests its guides or puts them in your route.
