- Polestar Experimental comes with Polestar: guides for Forever's new zones
  while they are still being written and tested (alpha): Riverglades for
  both factions, Shen'dralas and Mount Hyjal. They are on for everyone and
  show in the guide menu with our own guides, under Leveling in their level
  band. In the list each carries a small Forever mark (hover it for what the
  guide is); its details show a purple Experimental chip. Polestar never
  suggests them, never picks them for you and never puts them in your route:
  you open one from the guide menu or choose its zone where a route offers
  it. Options > Guide > Show experimental guides takes them out of the guide
  menu.
