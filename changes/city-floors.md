- Capitals: walks in Stormwind, Ironforge, Orgrimmar, the Undercity, Thunder
  Bluff and Darnassus no longer start on the ground under the streets or on
  the mountain over Ironforge when the arrow doesn't yet know your floor (after
  a loading screen, for example): it takes the floor from where players walk
  and from an NPC you just talked to or targeted. A trainer or vendor whose
  last few steps the walking maps don't reach (Ironforge's cooking trainer)
  now gets a walk to the nearest spot on their level, with "The last steps
  here aren't mapped" under the arrow, instead of a long walk out of the city
  and back (#2860).
