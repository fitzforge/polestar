- A character who installs Polestar part-way through a guide (or logs in with
  quest history) is put where their turned-in quests reach: one quest skipped
  early on no longer sends them back to it. Quests passed that way are listed
  as "N quests you skipped earlier" in the guide window, each to take back
  into the route or dismiss. The guide suggested at login now favours the one
  with the most of its quests done or in the log, and leaves out one that is
  all done (#86).
