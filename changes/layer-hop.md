section: players
---
- Layer hop: see which layer of the zone you are on, ask Polestar players on
  another layer for a group invite, and take the game's world refresh by a
  click or by itself. A request goes out only when you click: a line on a
  hidden Polestar channel and hidden whispers to the players in your zone.
  Hosts answer with an invite, other Polestar players with their layer when
  you refresh the list; nothing is sent by itself. A window you can drag and
  resize shows your layer, the hop in progress, how many players were asked
  and the layers other Polestar players reported. Off by default: turn it on
  under Quality of Life, Layers, then open it with /polestar layer.
