- Layer hop: see which layer of the zone you are on, ask Polestar players on
  another layer for a group invite, and take the game's world refresh by a
  click or by itself. A window you can drag and resize shows your layer, the
  hop in progress and the layers other Polestar players reported. Off by
  default: turn it on under Quality of Life, Layers, then open it with
  /polestar layer.
