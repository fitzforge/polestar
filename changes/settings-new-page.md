- Settings: with "New settings page" on, the window shows its settings in
  groups: a sidebar of Guiding, Look, Setup and About with pages under them,
  pinned settings and your profile at its foot, a folder tab for each tab of a
  page, a search-first header with Reload UI and a menu for Menu scale, Move
  frames and the resets, an All / Changed filter, and the live preview of a
  page beside its settings. Switch it off for the page as it was.
