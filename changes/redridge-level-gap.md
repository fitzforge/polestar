- Redridge Mountains (Alliance) no longer leaves you two levels short of
  Duskwood (thanks to the player who reported it on Discord). Its quests
  ended at about level 21.6 while Duskwood's start at 24. Redridge now adds
  Blackrock Blockade, a written top-up to level 23 at the Blackrock camps,
  and then Galardell Valley (Solomon's Law, the Old Lion Statue) and Bellygrub
  at a level that fits. Duskwood ends with a second Redridge pass at about
  25-26 for Tharil'zun, Shadow Magic, Gath'Ilzogg, Incinerator Gar'im and
  Lieutenant Fangore, with a tip to group up for the elites, and asks for
  level 23 before Worgen in the Woods.
