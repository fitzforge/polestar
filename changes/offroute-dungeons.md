- The quests that "aren't part of your route" can leave dungeon quests out:
  turn off Guide window > Parts > Quest log notices > Count dungeon quests,
  and a dungeon's quest, or one that leads to it (the quests with the dungeon
  mark), is no longer counted in that line, on the guide's end card or in its
  list. They stay in your log for a later run (thanks imp).
