- Group quests: "Do it with a group" is no longer final. At that quest's step
  the guide window offers "No group? Skip it", which skips it with the usual
  Undo; close the notice to keep it away for that quest this session.
- An elite in the step the guide is at (a kill marked "Elite: group
  advised", Overmaster Pyron for Disharmony of Flame) offers the same "No
  group? Skip it": it skips the quest, with Undo. Work among elites ("Elites
  nearby") does not.
