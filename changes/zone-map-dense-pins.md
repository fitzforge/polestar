- The continent and world maps no longer fill up with Polestar's pins: rares,
  treasures, the spawn markers for your quests (and their legend) and a
  target's other spawn spots now show on zone maps and the minimap only, so
  zone names and levels stay readable. The route, the target and the zone
  levels stay on the continent map. "Rares on the world map" and "Treasures
  on the world map" are now "Rares on zone maps" and "Treasures on zone
  maps" (thanks krillep, reported on CurseForge).
