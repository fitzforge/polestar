- `sim run --party N` (also `robust`, `compare`, `consensus`, `weave`) scores
  a party of 1 to 5 doing the route together: Classic's group XP bonus split
  per member, faster kills, shared kill credit, each member's own quest drops
  and a premade party walking into a dungeon. `--elites` builds a group world
  that keeps the elite quests the solo filter drops.
