- Do this zone, then come back: when you spend half a minute in a zone your
  leveling guide comes to later, the guide window offers to do that zone now.
  Before anything changes it shows what waits for the steps you pass over,
  quests above your level and anything those steps could lose. The guide then
  leads you through the zone and comes back to the steps you passed over, with
  nothing skipped. Also from a right-click on a step in Up next, and "Back now"
  ends it early. Options > Guide > Automation > Guides, "Zones ahead of the
  guide". (thanks Xaden Ryan)
