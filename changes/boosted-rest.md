- The guide window's XP line says how long your campfire Boosted Rest lasts
  ("Boosted Rest: 42m") and, when you carry the Cozy Sleeping Bag, whether it
  is ready or how long until it is. Party members who run Polestar show their
  Boosted Rest in the party panel. Turn it off under Options > Quality of
  Life > Trackers > Boosted Rest status.
