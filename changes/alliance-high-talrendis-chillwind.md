- In Azshara, pick up the Talrendis errands before hunting shrine spirits. Gather Berdun's honey on the way to Eldarath and bring it back with Truesong's relics. A priest called to Ogtinc takes the shrine road instead of riding there from the wasp nests; Berdun still takes honey that's ready. If you missed the bloodmages in the Hinterlands guide, you can take their beast requests before the Blasted Lands hunt.
- In Western Plaguelands, take the southern road to Chillwind before returning to Chromie's inn, and give MacDonnell the Writhing Haunt report there if you have it. Skip this trip if you have neither the report nor business with Chromie. In Un'Goro, gather the volcanic ash on the same climb as Krakle's thermometer.
