- In Azshara, pick up the Talrendis errands before hunting shrine spirits. Gather Berdun's honey on the way to Eldarath and bring it back with Truesong's relics. If you missed the bloodmages in the Hinterlands guide, you can take their beast requests before the Blasted Lands hunt.
- In Western Plaguelands, go straight from Mulgris to Chromie. Take the cauldron report to Chillwind with the skeletal fragments before you fly. In Un'Goro, gather the volcanic ash on the same climb as Krakle's thermometer.
