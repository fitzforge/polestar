- The Barrens: the Plainstrider Beaks no longer have a step of their own
  right after the Crossroads. About one plainstrider in four drops a beak, so
  you collect them on the way: the guide window shows your count under the
  current step from the Crossroads to Sergra. You hand them in on the stop
  after the Razormane camp. If you are still short then, one step sends you
  to the plains north-west of the Crossroads first; if you already have
  seven, it never comes up. (thanks Fitz)
- Ticking a quest goal done by hand no longer treats the quest as complete.
  The guide moves on as before, but the quest's hand-in waits until the game
  counts the objective, says why in its line, and the arrow no longer leads
  you to the quest giver early.
