- Alliance 12-20: Darkshore is now played in two parts around a trip to the
  Eastern Kingdoms at 18-20: Loch Modan from the north (the dam, the loch's
  toolbox, Ironband's Excavation, the Farstrider Lodge's hunts, Ylva's
  banner), out through Ironforge, then Redridge's 15-20 quests while they
  still give full XP, the Defias chain from Wiley the Black through Sentinel
  Hill, Stormwind and Moonbrook, and the lighthouse's Old Murk-Eye; the
  Hearthstone takes you back to Auberdine for Darkshore's southern loops and
  Kerlonian's escort into Ashenvale. Dwarves and Gnomes
  walk through east Elwynn on the way to Westfall (Guard Thomas, the Eastvale
  Logging Camp, the Tower of Azora, the Jasperlode Mine, Westbrook's gnoll
  bounty). Ashenvale no longer starts with a grind, and Redridge at 24 keeps
  its Blackrock quests. In Westfall, Humans take Scout Galiaan's quest beside
  Thor and walk Stormwind's errand in a shorter order.
