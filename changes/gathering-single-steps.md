- Mining, Herbalism, Skinning and Fishing guides now give one clear action per
  step: learn the profession, buy the tool, turn on tracking, each trainer
  rank when it is due, and each stretch of gathering with where to go and the
  skill that finishes it. Mining smelts where smelting raises the skill, with
  the forge pinned; Fishing walks you through the pole, lures, the Expert book
  and Nat Pagle's quest.
- New Lockpicking guide for Rogues (1-300), with the character level each
  stretch needs.
- New Camping guide: the campfire, your profession's camp item and an hour of
  Camp Benefits.
- The Supply Factions reputation guide is rebuilt the same way: one crate, the
  Shipping Label, writs one customer at a time, and each standing's reward.
