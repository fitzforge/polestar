- Recorder: a new explorer mode, off unless you type `/psrec explore on`. In
  Forever's new zones and near the new dungeons it notes the creatures there
  and the way you walk in more detail, and while it is on it also notes the
  quest log's map points, dungeon floors, boss pulls, the area each quest
  giver stands in and the chests and objects you open. It's all about the
  game world: nothing new about you. `/psrec explore off` stops it.
