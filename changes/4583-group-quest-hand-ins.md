- Leveling guides: the hand-in of a group quest (an elite's bounty such as
  WANTED: Scoutmaster Vargas in Riverglades) waits until the quest is done, so
  a player who passed the group work is no longer sent to hand it in
  unfinished; fourteen more such hand-ins in Riverglades, Azshara, Silithus,
  the Burning Steppes and Mount Hyjal wait the same way (thanks king_iix).
