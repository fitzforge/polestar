- The quest room's "Hand in your finished quests" guide now includes every
  finished quest in your log, including those another guide hands in. Open
  it directly from the guide window's menu; it is greyed out when none are ready.
  Quests at the same ender share a stop, plain deliveries are included,
  and the order accounts for estimated travel time. Known destinations keep
  their waypoints without a travel estimate; unknown locations are named
  (thanks Swatto).
