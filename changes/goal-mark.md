- Guide format 10: add `mark <npc> <icon> ["note"]` inside a step to put a
  raid icon (`skull`, `cross`, `square`, `moon`, `triangle`, `diamond`,
  `circle`, `star` or 1-8) over that mob's nameplates while the step is
  current, with the note on its tooltip. A mark never holds the step. Only
  the player sees the icon; Polestar never sets a raid target. Players can
  turn the icons off under Guide > Arrow & routes > Along the route. Needs
  Polestar 0.3.7.
