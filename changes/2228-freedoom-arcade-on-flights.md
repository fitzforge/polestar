- Freedoom Arcade by petllama comes with Polestar: on a flight with a minute
  or more to go, a small card under the flight timer asks "Do you want to play
  Doom while waiting?". Play opens three levels of Freedoom in a window in
  your Polestar look, loaded only then. 10 seconds before you land the game
  is saved, paused and closed so your keys steer again, and /doom carries on
  where you were. With petllama's own Freedoom Arcade addon installed,
  Polestar opens that one, all 36 levels. /polestar arcade and the guide
  window's menu open it any time, and Integrations > Freedoom Arcade turns
  the offer off (thanks petllama, #2228).
