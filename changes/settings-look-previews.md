- The Look pages' previews are now a large scene across the top of the
  page: the real guide window, cards, alerts, buttons, maps and the arrow,
  drawn at their true size side by side over the zone you stand in, each on
  a soft shadow. The Theme page shows the guide window with its heads-up
  card, a rare alert, the arrow and the buttons together; its Names tab
  shows every kind of name in its colour.
- The Buttons page shows the buttons over the world they act on: the mob
  the target button names, marked when Mark target is on, and the rare the
  rare button names. The rare alert and the transport alert come up at the
  top of the world with the arrow under them and the guide window at the
  side.
- Point at a setting and the parts of the preview it changes light up. On
  the Theme page, the settings that dress the settings window itself (its
  sidebar, border, header band, haze and rim) light those parts too, and
  the accent lights everything drawn in it: tabs, progress bars, buttons,
  the arrow, the cards' stripes and the settings window's own switches,
  sliders and chips.
- The Alerts & toasts and Trackers pages open on every alert or tracker at
  once, the journey included; with "Line up alerts" on, the alerts stand in
  their line.
