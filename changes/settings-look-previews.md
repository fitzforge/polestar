- The Look tabs' previews now show just what the tab sets, at its real size,
  centred across the top of the page and only as tall as it needs: the
  arrow on its way, closing in and in combat; the action buttons as you laid
  them out; each alert alone. Tabs that set several things pick the one to
  show from a dropdown.
- The Map pins page shows a sharp piece of the world map and the minimap
  side by side, with your pins on both.
- The Theme page shows the three things it dresses side by side: the guide
  window, the heads-up card and a rare alert. On a narrow window it shows
  them smaller and says by how much; its Names tab shows every kind of name
  in its colour.
- Each alert's tab has a Play button beside the alert to hear its sound.
- Point at a setting and the parts of the preview it changes light up. On
  the Theme page, the settings that dress the settings window itself (its
  sidebar, border, header band, haze and rim) light those parts too, and
  the accent lights everything drawn in it: tabs, progress bars, buttons,
  the cards' stripes and the settings window's own switches, sliders and
  chips.
- The Buttons page's Action buttons tab shows the buttons over the world
  they act on: the mob the target button names, marked when Mark target is
  on, and the rare the rare button names.
- The Trackers page opens on every tracker at once, the journey included.
