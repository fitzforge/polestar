- The action buttons can dock to the guide window: Settings > Buttons >
  Action buttons > "Dock to window" (off by default) lays them in a row along
  the window's bottom that moves, grows and hides with it. The target button
  (or the target list's mark, its list hanging under the window) and the rare
  button sit at the left with the name beside them; the quest item,
  Hearthstone and bag sit at the right end. With the single target button,
  the goal it takes next wears its crosshair in the window. In a fight the
  row stays put and catches up when the fight ends (#2242, thanks Vesper,
  #3086).
