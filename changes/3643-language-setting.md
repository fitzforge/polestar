- Polestar can show another language than your game's: `/polestar language enUS`
  for English on a German (or any other) game, `/polestar language deDE` for
  German on an English one, `/polestar language game` to follow the game
  again. It takes effect after a `/reload`; key binding names and the
  settings' labels stay in your game's language. A row in Options > General
  comes later. Turning a language folder off in the AddOns list no longer
  prints a chat line at every login; only a folder missing from the AddOns
  folder does. (thanks to the German players who asked on CurseForge, #3643)
