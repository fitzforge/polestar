- New zones on your route: where a leveling guide ends next to one of
  Forever's new zones, "Choose your path" now offers it beside the usual zone,
  marked experimental and never recommended: the Riverglades after the
  Badlands (Horde, about level 40), Shen'dralas after Feralas (Horde, about
  46) and Mount Hyjal after the Western Plaguelands (Alliance, about 58).
  There is nothing to install or switch on: choosing it loads the experimental
  guides, and the zone's last guide hands you back to the usual route. With
  "Take the recommended zone" on, your route stays as it was.
