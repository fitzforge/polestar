- Dungeons in the route (Alliance): with a dungeon in your route, the guide
  now takes every quest of it you can do, prerequisites included, unless you
  choose "Only quests on my way" for that dungeon. New: Speak with Shoni,
  Underground Assault, Collecting Memories and Oh Brother... for the
  Deadmines; What Comes Around... and The Color of Blood for the Stockade; The
  Corruption Abroad and the Darnassus quests In Search of Thaelrid, Twilight
  Falls and Blackfathom Villainy for Blackfathom Deeps; Seeking Caitlin, Lost
  in the Thicket Things, Heartwoven, Songblade Search and Fallen in the Fen
  for the Excavation Site; Friend of the Kirin Tor, A Taste of Home and
  Thicker Than Water around Dalaran; A Sign of Hope, Amulet of Secrets and
  Power Stones for Uldaman; Jammal'an the Prophet, The Essence of Eranikus,
  Haze of Evil, and Marvon Rivetseeker's Into the Depths and Secret of the
  Circle (his chain now comes before the run) for the Sunken Temple; Screecher
  Spirits, The Prophecy of Mosh'aru, Tabetha's Task and Tiara of the Deep for
  Zul'Farrak. Class quests count too: Mages get the Celestial Orb chain up to
  Power in Uldaman, A Lesson in Literacy and Destroy Morphaz, Priests Blood of
  Morphaz, Hunters The Hunter's Charm chain to The Green Drake, and Rogues A
  Simple Request to The Azure Key. A dungeon quest you couldn't finish is
  dropped where the route last passes its hand-in, so none stays in your log
  to 60, and a player who hearths back to Auberdine at the end of Loch Modan
  hands the Unsent Letter in on the next Stormwind stop.
