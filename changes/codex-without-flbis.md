- The Codex behind Lookup, Nearby services and the Dungeons pages is rebuilt
  from our own and openly licensed sources and catches up with this month's
  fixes: Nearby services now finds food and drink, arrow, bullet, bow and gun
  and general goods vendors, pick-up guides follow quest chains such as The
  Wrath of Rath'mael's, dungeon pages show each quest's XP, Onyxia's
  attunement can be guided, lookups leave out stray spawns, and your own
  race's vendor mounts no longer ask for Exalted. The Forever-only racial
  mounts (the Reins of the Striped Dawnsaber, the White Kodo, the Black
  Skeletal Horse) now name their vendor.
