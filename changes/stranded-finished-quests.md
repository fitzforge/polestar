- Finished quests the route no longer hands in (taken off the route, left in
  a guide you jumped past, or from before an update re-ordered the zones) no
  longer sit in your log unnoticed: the guide window's "Still to do" list
  says how many you carry and what they are worth, and a click lists each
  with who takes it in and where, a "Guide me" that leads you to them,
  nearest first, and an Abandon button on the grey ones (thanks jaff).
- While you're here now offers those hand-ins too when you pass the quest's
  NPC, not only the hand-ins of later steps of the guide you're in.
- "You have outlevelled this guide" says when the guide (or the guides the
  jump skips) still hands in finished quests you carry ("2 finished quests
  here first"), and the jump asks once; it suggests the next guide instead
  of the level-best one while those guides still have your hand-ins. The
  guide menu asks the same before loading another guide over yours.
