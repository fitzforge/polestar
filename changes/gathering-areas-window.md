- Herbalism, Mining and Skinning: the profession window's Pick it, Mine it and
  Kill and skin it list up to three areas safe at your level, best first:
  about how many herbs, veins or beasts (and their levels) are up there, the
  level each is safe from and how far it is, each with Guide me. Your pick is
  kept for that step until the area is gathered out, when the window offers
  the next one to try; with no area safe yet it says from which level one is.
  The zone map shades the area the arrow leads to and dots its nodes, the
  minimap shows the dots, and once you are in it the arrow says what to roam
  for and which tracking shows it on your minimap.
