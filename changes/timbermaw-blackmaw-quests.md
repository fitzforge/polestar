- Timbermaw Hold Reputation: once the Timbermaw are Neutral to you, the guide
  takes you to Ursolan in Azshara for the Blackmaw furbolgs' quests, then to
  Gorn One Eye in Timbermaw Hold: about 50,000 experience below level 60.
  Below Neutral the Blackmaw attack you, so the leveling routes, which reach
  Azshara long before Neutral, leave them out.
