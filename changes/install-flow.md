- The welcome a new character gets is now Install: a few short pages that set
  Polestar up for how you play, each choice working the moment you make it,
  with no reload. Questing (quest automation, grey quests, the guide at login
  and the stat weights gear advice scores rewards with), Targeting (the
  target button, the square button with a name beside it that targets your
  step's mobs and NPCs, shown with a preview, then who it targets, its mark
  and its ping), Groups (group quests), Your route (the route style and the
  route line) and Your look (Polestar's looks, the game's and the skins, with
  a preview, and the colour scheme). "Install · Step 2 of 7" shows where you
  are, Skip install keeps everything as it is, and Enter, Backspace and
  Escape work as well as the gamepad. A later character gets only its own
  choice, its class's stat weights. Open it again with the Install button in
  the settings window's header, About's Install, the settings search
  ("install", "setup", "wizard") or `/polestar install`.
