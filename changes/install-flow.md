- The welcome a new character gets is now Install: a few short pages that set
  Polestar up for how you play, each choice working the moment you make it,
  with no reload. Questing (quest automation, grey quests, the guide at login
  and the stat weights gear advice scores rewards with), Targeting (the
  target button, which lists your step's mobs and NPCs so a click targets one,
  shown with a preview; the list goes with the button and has no switch of its
  own; Simple target button brings back the single square button and who it
  targets; then its mark and its ping), Groups and dungeons (dungeons in your route, group quests),
  Your route (the route style and the route line) and Your look (Polestar's
  looks, the game's and the skins, with a preview, and the colour scheme).
  "Install · Step 2 of 7" shows where you are, Skip install keeps everything
  as it is, and Enter, Backspace and Escape work as well as the gamepad. A
  later character gets only its own choice, its class's stat weights. Open it
  again with the Install button in the settings window's header, About's
  Install, the settings search ("install", "setup", "wizard") or
  `/polestar install`.
