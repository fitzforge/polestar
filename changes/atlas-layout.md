- Atlas, an optional layout for the guide window, in its own folder
  (Polestar_Layout_Atlas) and its own card in Options > Guide window >
  Presets: the step as a node on a timeline with its goals one line each,
  Up next on a rail that fades ahead (folded to its heading with Expand Up
  next off), the tip under the goals, the XP row on the timeline's columns,
  and While you're here, Still to do, Optional and Training due as cards
  above the window. The row the arrow points at is lit by a soft wash in
  your target bar colour, on the step and on the cards alike. Picking the
  Atlas card chooses the layout only: your skin stays yours. Polestar's own
  layout stays the default. Polestar_Skin_Atlas has the Atlas colours as a
  skin of its own.
- Guide window: settings the Atlas layout starts from, each off or as before
  by default: Steps before (0 to 5 dimmed lines over the step, marked done or
  skipped; click one to go back), an All steps button in the step bar, a
  Journey style (a layout may offer more than under the step) and a notice's
  action on its first line. A layout may also default the step number's size
  and the step card's gaps (over and under the step's title, over and round
  the tip, Up next's and the steps before's rows, the XP row).
