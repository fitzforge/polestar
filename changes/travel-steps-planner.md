  "Zeppelin to the Undercity") now says where to get to and lets the arrow's
  route pick how: it reads "Hearth to Everlook" when your Hearthstone is bound
  there, "Take the boat to Auberdine", or "Hearth to Camp Taurajo, then fly to
  Zoram'gar Outpost", and getting there any way finishes it. The Hearthstone is
  kept when the guide comes back to your inn later. Mount Hyjal's "Fly to
  Everlook" and "Fly to Moonglade" led nowhere before: no arrow at all. (thanks
  Soggy)
