- Guide Planner: a new button in the guide menu's details shows the selected
  guide as a timeline, step by step, whether it is open or not: what is done,
  where you are, what comes. Clear a quest's tick to leave it out of your
  route: the quests that need it go with it, steps with nothing left drop out,
  and the summary says how much quest XP that costs. Set the tick, or Show
  all, to bring them back. (thanks mcclaskiem)
