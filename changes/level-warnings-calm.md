section: players
---
- The guide no longer reads like you're falling behind. A step where you
  only take a quest never shows a quest-level warning: taking a higher
  quest early is normal. A quest you can't take yet isn't listed as a task
  with "Requires level"; the guide carries on and brings it back when you
  reach the level. The pace line says "Behind" only when you're more than a
  level under the route, and a hard quest is named calmly, only on the step
  where you fight it.
- A guide no longer skips its travel and training because you carry a
  quest from an earlier visit to the zone.
