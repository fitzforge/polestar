- The guide no longer reads like you're falling behind. A step where you
  only take a quest never shows a quest-level warning: taking a higher
  quest early is normal. When a quest you can take is less than a level
  away, the guide waits on that step and says "Requires level 29: 0.3 levels
  to go"; the rest of the step stays doable. Further away, it moves on to
  the next step only. The pace line says "Behind" only when you're more than
  a level under the route, and a hard quest is named calmly, only on the
  step where you fight it.
