- Horde 40-60 now goes through the zones in the order their quest levels fit:
  - Booty Bay's hand-ins on arrival, then the Swamp of Sorrows' 35-43 quests
    first (through Duskwood from Nesingwary's camp), then Stranglethorn's
    40-42 quests around Grom'gol and Zul'Mamwe.
  - Tanaris, Dustwallow Marsh (Brackenwall's chain) and Feralas at 42-45.
  - A second Stranglethorn visit at 45 for its 44-46 quests (the Bloodsail
    chain, Mistvale and Mokk, Zanzil, Speaking with Gan'zulah and the Fate of
    Yenniku, which used to wait until 50), with the swamp's Misty Reed Post
    quests right after.
  - Tanaris' main visit, the Hinterlands (leaving by Tarren Mill and the
    Undercity for Venom Bottles and Oran Snakewrithe), then Feralas' last
    quests.
  - The Searing Gorge and the Burning Steppes on one boat trip from Tanaris,
    with the Blasted Lands and Booty Bay on the way back (50-52), then
    Azshara and Un'Goro.
- New on the route: Continued Threat at Misty Reed Post, and Whiskey Slim's
  Lost Grog (the bottles on the Hinterlands beaches, handed in at Booty Bay).
