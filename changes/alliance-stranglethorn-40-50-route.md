- Stranglethorn Vale (Alliance, 40-50): the optional Cortello's scroll step is
  gone. The scroll lay on the Bloodsail ships south of Booty Bay among pirates
  three to five levels above you at 40, and the rest of the chain led away
  from the route. Hostile Takeover moves here from the 30-40 loops: Kebok
  offers it upstairs in Booty Bay with his tusk errand. If you already have the
  eight crystals, hand them in there. Otherwise, gather them at Lake Nazferiti
  around level 40–41 and bring them back with the tusks on the last town visit. Akiris by the Bundle,
  Zanzil's Secret, Voodoo Dues and The Bloodsail Buccaneers are no longer
  handed to you at 40 to carry for twenty hours: the level-48 Booty Bay stop
  that does them gives them, and the Riverglades quest log stays lighter. Thanks to Twerkjob and Crest for the route feedback.
- On the later Stranglethorn visit, stop for Sin'Dall only if you still need her paw. If you already have it, go straight to Ajeck with the other hunters' hand-ins.
- In Searing Gorge, Culling the Competition is a group choice: the Slavers fight in packs. Solo players can get Velarok's daggers from the same surface furnaces as the Smithing Tuyere. The Riverglades guide warns early arrivals about the Western Front before they reach its elite patrols. On the later Bloodsail visit, get the Golden Spyglass from Jon-Jon on Maury’s hill, not at Chucky’s camp.
