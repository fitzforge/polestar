- When you reach a dungeon that gets a party of your size to level 60 sooner,
  by Polestar's route simulator, the guide window says so: about how many
  minutes it saves and in what share of the simulator's runs. "Pick up its
  quests" opens a guide that collects them on the way to the entrance. Pick
  "Ask me", "Open the pick-up guide" or "Off" in Options, Discovery,
  Dungeons, "Suggest dungeons".
