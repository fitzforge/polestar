- Two new guide packs ship with Polestar, off until you switch one on (guide
  menu > Community guides): Polestar Completionist Guides (Alliance) and
  (Horde). Each takes every quest of its faction that our data can place, zone
  by zone to level 60, with a guide for each dungeon, raid, attunement, class
  and seasonal event. They are drafted from the quest data and checked by the
  simulated playthrough, and not yet played: expect rough routes, and report
  what is wrong.
