- Two new guide packs ship with Polestar, off until you switch one on (guide
  menu > Community guides): Polestar Completionist Guides (Alliance) and
  (Horde). Each takes every quest of its faction that our data can place, in
  every zone, grouped by zone in the order of the quests' levels, with a guide
  for each dungeon, raid, attunement, class and seasonal event. They are not a
  leveling route: doing all of it out-levels each zone. They are drafted from
  the quest data and checked by the simulated playthrough, and not yet played:
  expect rough routes, and report what is wrong.
