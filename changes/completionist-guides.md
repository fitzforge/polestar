- Polestar Completionist, a guide pack of its own (the addon
  PolestarPack_Completionist, a separate download): for every race, a chain
  of guides through every open-world quest the character can take, levels 1
  to 60, the other races' starting zones included, in a Completionist
  category of the guide menu. It goes zone by zone: each visit does every
  quest you can take there at your level, and the quests you are too low for
  come back as a later visit. Elite quests are optional goals that say the
  elites' level ("Hogger (level 11) is elite: grouping up is advised unless
  you are well above level 11."). Class quests stay with the Class guides and
  dungeon quests with the Dungeons guides. A Completionist guide's details
  say how many of its quests this character has already turned in.
