- Dungeons in the leveling route: The Deadmines, The Stockade (Alliance),
  Wailing Caverns and Blackfathom Deeps (Horde). For a character who runs
  them (Dungeons in my route, or Options, Discovery, Dungeons for one), the
  guides pick up their quests on the stops they already make, send you to
  the door at the right level with what to do inside, and hand them in on
  the next stops. Skipped, the default, the route stays as it is.
