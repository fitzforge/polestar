  (Alliance), Wailing Caverns or Blackfathom Deeps (Horde) in Options,
  Discovery, Dungeons, "Dungeons in leveling guides", and the guides pick up
  their quests on the stops they already make, send you to the door at the
  right level with what to do inside, and hand them in on the next stops.
  Without a choice the route stays as it is.
