- Dungeons in the leveling route: The Deadmines, The Stockade (Alliance),
  Wailing Caverns and Blackfathom Deeps (Horde). For a character who runs
  them (Dungeons in my route), the guides pick up their quests on the stops
  they already make, send you to the door at the right level with what to do
  inside, and hand them in on the next stops. Skipped, the route stays as it
  is.
