- `forge guide lint` flags a tip in Polestar's own guides that tells the
  player to do something (use, right-click or read an item, click or open an
  object, wear something, talk to someone, an emote) that no goal row of the
  step does. Give the action its own row; a real false positive takes
  `# tip-ok: <why>` on the line above the tip (docs/GUIDE_FORMAT.md, `tip`).
