- A pack can rename its guides: `renamed.json` in the pack folder maps each
  old guide id to its new one, and Polestar moves a player's save to the new
  ids at login, so their tabs open at the same step with every check-off and
  skip. Another pack's `next` to an old id leads to the new one
  (docs/PACK_FORMAT.md, "Renamed guides").
