- Nine new skins, each a small addon of its own in the download: enable the
  ones you like in the AddOns list and pick them under Settings > Theme.
  Vellum (a page of the quest log: dark ink on parchment), Lumen (head-up:
  no frame, a cyan line and corner ticks), Beacon (high contrast: black,
  white and yellow, bigger type, arrow and pins), Aurora (Polestar's night
  sky a step further), Hairline (flat and pixel-exact, your class colour as
  the accent), Umbra (dark mode, on Polestar's frames or on the game's own
  art dimmed), Waymark (the continent you're levelling in behind the
  header, its colour on the accent, the arrow and the pins), Lanterns (ten
  moods, each a pair of colours with two soft lights) and Sheen (soft
  glass, your class colour running into the accent).
