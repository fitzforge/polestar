- New skins, each a small addon of its own in the download: turn on the ones
  you like in the game's AddOns list and pick them under Settings > Theme.
  Vellum (a page of the quest log: dark ink on parchment), Lumen (head-up:
  no frame, a cyan line and corner ticks over the world), Beacon (high
  contrast: black, white and yellow, bigger type, arrow and pins) and Aurora
  (Polestar's night sky a step further: an aurora in the band, champagne
  gold).
