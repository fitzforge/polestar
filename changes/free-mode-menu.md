- Free mode has a page in the main window, right under Guides: Start (Return to
  your guide while it is on), which quest first, and every quest in your log
  with a tick (in the loop or left out), a pin, its zone, level and "Ready to
  hand in". Leave out a whole zone or the group quests in one go, bring them
  back with one more. Your choices stay with the character. "Hand in finished
  quests (N)" opens the shortest trip through your hand-ins from there too.
  Click a quest's pin or objective area on the world map to make it Free
  mode's first quest, and again to unpin it. Free mode has its own icon, a
  dotted route winding to a "!".
- Import moved into Settings, Community guides, and the guide menu's Community
  guides list ends with "+ Import a guide". The Import page in the main
  window is gone; `/polestar import` opens the new place.
- A slim row in the guide window, "N quests ready to hand in", when two or more
  quests in your log are finished and none is part of this step. Route opens the
  hand-in guide, the close button hides it until the number changes, and
  Settings, Look, Guide window, Parts, Hand-in row turns it off.
