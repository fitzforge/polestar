- Free mode has a page in the main window, right under Guides, laid out for
  why you came. Never tried it: one big "Activate Free mode" at the centre,
  under your own quests drawn as a constellation: the Polestar star leads a
  dotted path through a star for each of the first three quests in Free mode's
  order, each named with why it comes there; under the button, what Free mode
  is (you pick up quests, Polestar plans the order, the arrow leads). Tried it
  before: the same sky, Activate and the order under it, then the first three
  quests. On: your
  route, numbered in the order the arrow takes it, the quest it leads to now
  marked, each with why it's there, its progress, the walk, its XP and zone;
  finished quests stand under the NPC who takes them. Hover a quest (or focus
  it with a gamepad) for the game's quest tooltip with where, why and its
  objectives. "Back to <your guide>" always names your leveling guide, never
  the hand-in route. "Hand in finished quests (N)" shows how long the trip
  takes and opens the shortest route. Quests you leave out fold away under
  Left out with Bring back; leave out a whole zone or the group quests in one
  go. Your choices stay with the character. Click a quest's pin or objective
  area on the world map to make it Free mode's first quest, and again to unpin
  it. Free mode's icon is a running figure, on its menu item, its tab and its
  settings.
- In Free mode the guide window says so once, when it starts ("You're in Free
  mode: your own quests, planned for you."), and the step shows why its quest
  comes first in one line under the header. The way back and the order are in
  the window's menu.
- Import moved into Settings, Community guides, and the guide menu's Community
  guides list ends with "+ Import a guide". The Import page in the main
  window is gone; `/polestar import` opens the new place.
- A slim row in the guide window, "N quests ready to hand in", when two or more
  quests in your log are finished and none is part of this step. Route opens the
  hand-in guide, the close button hides it until the number changes, and
  Settings, Look, Guide window, Parts, Hand-in row turns it off.
