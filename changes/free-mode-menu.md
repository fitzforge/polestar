- Free mode has its own place in the main window, right under Guides, with
  three pages in its sidebar, the same whether it's on or off. Main page is
  where you turn it on and off: your own quests drawn as a constellation (the
  Polestar star leads a dotted path through a star for each of the first three
  quests in Free mode's order, each named with why it comes there) over one big
  "Activate Free mode"; while it's on, the same page says "Free mode is on ·
  following N quests" over one "Turn off Free mode", with the guide it takes
  you back to written under it. Your quests lists every quest in the order Free
  mode takes them (or would take them, while it's off), the quest the arrow
  leads to now marked, each with why it's there, its progress, the walk, its
  XP and zone; finished quests stand under the NPC who takes them. Which quest
  first and "Hand in finished quests (N)" (how long the trip takes, and the
  shortest route) are there too. Hover a quest (or focus it with a gamepad)
  for the game's quest tooltip with where, why and its objectives. Quests you
  leave out fold away under Left out with Bring back; leave out a whole zone
  or the group quests in one go. About Free mode shows how it works in four
  drawn steps, when to use it and how to get back to your guide. Turning it
  off always takes you back to your leveling guide, never the hand-in route.
  Your choices stay with the character. While Free mode is on, click a quest's
  pin or objective area on the world map to make it Free mode's first quest,
  and again to unpin it. Free mode's icon is a running figure, on its menu
  item, its tab and its settings; Settings' Free mode button opens its Main
  page.
- In Free mode the guide window says so once, when it starts ("You're in Free
  mode: your own quests, planned for you."), and the step shows why its quest
  comes first in one line under the header. The window's menu has "Turn on
  Free mode" and, while it's on, "Turn off Free mode" (naming the guide it
  takes you back to) and the order.
- Import moved into Settings, Community guides, and the guide menu's Community
  guides list ends with "+ Import a guide". The Import page in the main
  window is gone; `/polestar import` opens the new place.
