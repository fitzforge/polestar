- Group play: party sync is now on in a party (turn it off under Options,
  Questing, Group), and the first time you group with another Polestar user
  it says so once. When you finish an objective a party member
  on the same quest still needs, the step and its turn-in wait for them
  ("Waiting on Ana: 3/8 Boar Ribs", then "Everyone's ready") and the arrow
  leads to what they still need; a member in another zone or offline for over
  2 minutes doesn't hold you, and "Go on without them" moves on. A Share
  button on a quest you have that a member lacks, with the reason when they
  can't take it; a party panel (click the party line or /polestar party) with
  each member's level, step and what they still need; and "Ana still needs 2"
  on collect goals and on loot a member needs and you don't.
