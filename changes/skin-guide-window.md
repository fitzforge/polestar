- Skins reach further into the guide window: `fontSizes` sizes the step
  card's own text (`cardName`, `cardGoal`, `cardNote`), a `guideTitle` hook
  shows a title bar and a close square in place of the starlit band (tokens
  `closeFill`, `closeEdge`, `closeGlyph`; capability `surfaces.guideTitle`),
  and a skin's `windowBorder` token edges its windows on every base, under
  the player's own border colour.
