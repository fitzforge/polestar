- Arrow (Horde): walks between the north and the south of the Eastern
  Kingdoms (into the Badlands from the Wetlands, between Tirisfal and Kargath
  or Stonard, from the Searing Gorge into the Hinterlands) keep to the road
  through the Dun Algaz pass instead of a walk round the coast past Stormwind
  up to three times as long. The arrow still names Dun Algaz as a hostile
  town on the way, and still takes you round Thelsamar.
