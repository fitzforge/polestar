- Timed quests show their clock: the guide window, in full and compact mode,
  puts the time left after each of the quest's goals ("4:12 left"), amber
  under two minutes and red under one, and says before you accept how long
  the quest allows. A chat line and a sound warn at 5 minutes, at 1 minute
  and if the quest fails (Questing > Quests > Timed quest alerts), and the
  step tells you when the way to the hand-in takes longer than the time
  left. While a timed quest runs, "While you're here" keeps showing that
  quest's own objectives and hand-in, first, and holds the rest until it is
  done. (Discord request)
