- Dynamic Questing can now point out dungeon quests as you pass their givers
  (Settings > Guide > Dynamic Questing > Dungeon quests you pass, off by
  default). It offers a dungeon's quest, or the first quest of its chain, that
  you can take now, for a dungeon up to 10 levels ahead, while your quest log
  has 5 free slots. The line says which dungeon it is for.
