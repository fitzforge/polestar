- In Durotar, the level 11 top-up stays away from Halikor. Look for patrol
  warnings at the Dead Field, the Ashenvale Outrunners and the Field of
  Giants, and advice for pulling the Ursera and Al'Aketh Assassins on Zephras
  Isle. Tauren and Horde Skyborne see how to make the incense for Hunter Rise,
  and Undead Paladins can track each conversation at Bandarion Keep.
