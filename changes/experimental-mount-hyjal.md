- Polestar Experimental now has Mount Hyjal (levels 56-59) for both
  factions, in three parts: the Tainted Foothills and Felblood Scar;
  Barkskin Burrow, Elderwild and the Cradle of Tranquility; then the Shrine
  of Aviana, Dae'gun and the Summit of Eternity. The zone's elite quests are
  a choice you make (do them with a group or skip them). It is written from
  Forever's quest data and the first recording of the zone, so some steps,
  and every Horde-only step, are not checked in game yet: the guide says so
  where it matters, and Polestar's Discord and GitHub take your corrections.
