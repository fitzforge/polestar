- Profession guides no longer ask how to get their materials up front: the
  "How do you want to get them?" card, the Materials window
  (`/polestar materials`), the guide window's Gather materials, Buy materials
  and Mix menu rows, and the Options default are gone. Each Get it step
  already offers its ways ("Pick one way"), so you pick there, per material,
  and the guide remembers it. Until you pick, a material you can gather at
  your level and with your professions is gathered and the rest is bought,
  as Mix did. Your earlier picks for single materials are kept; a whole-guide
  Gather all or Buy all goes back to that automatic way.
