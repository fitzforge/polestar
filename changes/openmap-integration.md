- OpenMap: with OpenMap's stitched world map, Polestar's pins, route lines
  and trail now show across every zone the view shows, above OpenMap's art.
  Polestar's map layers get switches in OpenMap's menu, its dungeon card
  lists Polestar's quests for that dungeon, and "Show on map" glides there.
- Map icons for dungeons, raids, flight masters, docks and inns now look
  like the game's own; route stops show the mode you change to.
- Route lines on the world map can move toward the goal, label each leg
  with its length and time, preview the next steps' goals, and show your
  whole travel network.
