- Five paired profession guides level two professions together, 1-300 each:
  Herbalism + Alchemy, Mining + Blacksmithing, Mining + Engineering,
  Skinning + Leatherworking and Tailoring + Enchanting. Each crafting stretch
  starts with a gathering loop sized to what that stretch needs, which also
  raises the gathering skill, and the craft follows with the materials in your
  bags; both trainers are visited in one trip when both ranks are due.
  Tailoring + Enchanting disenchants the green armour you tailor, and its
  Enchanting steps say how much of what they ask those crafts give.
