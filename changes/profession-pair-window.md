- A paired profession guide (Skinning + Leatherworking and the other pairs)
  shows both skills in its window: two small meters stacked, gathering first,
  each with its rank, the stretch still to go and a tick where it moves on;
  the compact window one line ("Skinning 72 · Leatherworking 51"), and Atlas
  says both in its XP slot. The step's "Skin 34 Light Leather ..., then craft
  45 Light Armor Kits ..." line is now the subtitle of the recipe's card, and
  "from your skinning on the way: 12 of 34" stands in the materials. The guide
  menu offers the nearest trainer of each skill, and the trainer line says
  which skill its next rank is of.
