- A paired profession guide (Skinning + Leatherworking and the other pairs)
  looks like its single guides: both skills on two slim lines stacked at the
  top, gathering first, each with its rank ("Not learned yet" before you
  learn it), in compact mode and in Atlas too, with no XP or pace line. A
  craft step is of its own skill: its colour, the crafts left
  and Open Leatherworking. The guide menu offers the nearest trainer of each
  skill.
