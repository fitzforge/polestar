- A paired profession guide (Skinning + Leatherworking and the other pairs)
  shows both skills in its window: two slim lines stacked, gathering first,
  each with its rank ("Not learned yet" before you learn it); the compact
  window one line ("Skinning 72 · Leatherworking 51"), and Atlas says both in
  its XP slot. A craft step is of its own skill: its colour, the crafts left
  and Open Leatherworking. The guide menu offers the nearest trainer of each
  skill.
