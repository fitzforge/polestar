**Polestar Client 0.1.9** clears out every addon folder Polestar 0.4.3 no longer uses when you update to it, without touching your skins. Update all now gives you one message, and a community guide's update badge shows on Community Guides.

Already have Polestar Client 0.1.4 or newer? Click **Update to 0.1.9** in the bar at the top of the window, and the app restarts on the new version. On 0.1.2 or older, look for it at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**).

## What's fixed

- **Updating to Polestar 0.4.3 removes the folders it folded.** Polestar 0.4.3 ships 22 addon folders instead of 45: its looks, skins, tab styles, experimental guides and the nine race guide parts now live inside `Polestar`, `Polestar_Guides_Alliance`, `Polestar_Guides_Horde` and the new `Polestar_Skins`. When Polestar Client updates Polestar, it now removes all 24 old folders, including the Atlas skin and layout folders from the 0.4.3 previews, so the game stops loading them. If you updated Polestar before updating the Client, click **Repair** on Polestar in the Addons page once to clear them. The example skin (`Polestar_Skin_Example`) and skins or layouts made by other people are never removed. Thanks willsarg (#3955, #3818).
- **Update all says it once.** After Update all, the message lists everything it installed in one sentence and asks you to restart the game (or /reload) once, not once per addon. Thanks willsarg (#3953) and Slugstur for the report (#3638).
- **The update badge for a community guide is on Community Guides.** The number next to **Addons** in the sidebar now counts only addon updates; a community guide's update is counted next to **Community Guides**, where you install it. **Update all** on the Addons page still installs both, and its tooltip now says so and sends you to Community Guides for updates that need you to confirm them first. Thanks willsarg (#3950) and Slugstur for the report (#3832).
