**Polestar Client 0.1.4** sends the new things the Recorder notes in Polestar's next update, and lets you look back at what was already sent.

Already have Polestar Client 0.1.3? Click **Update to 0.1.4** in the bar at the top of the window, and the app restarts on the new version. On 0.1.2 or older, look for it at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**).

## What's new

- **The Recorder's new notes.** Sends the new things the Recorder notes in Polestar's next update: herbs, ore, fishing pools and locks with the skill they needed, whether you were in a group, failed quests and "you can't do that yet" messages. Nothing about you that wasn't sent before. **What we send** names each of them.
- **Already sent.** **What we send** has an **Already sent** tab: the uploads of the last 8 days, newest first, each with the same summary and every event as the next upload.
