**Polestar Client 0.1.2** opens again on every Linux desktop.

Already have Polestar Client and it opens normally? It offers this update by itself: look for **Update to 0.1.2** at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**), click it, and the app restarts on the new version.

Did 0.1.1 open on an error screen for you? Then the update button can't be reached: download 0.1.2 from the table below and run it in place of the old one. Your settings are kept.

## What's fixed

- **The app opens instead of showing an error.** On some Linux desktops, 0.1.1 opened on "Something went wrong: Command plugin:notification|is_permission_granted not allowed by ACL" instead of its window. Fixed: the window now opens normally.

**First launch:**
- **Windows** may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** the app is signed and checked by Apple; open the .dmg, drag Polestar Client into Applications, and open it.
- **Linux AppImage:** make it executable (right-click > Properties > Allow executing, or `chmod +x`), then run it.

Something off? Tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/polestar) or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar/issues).
