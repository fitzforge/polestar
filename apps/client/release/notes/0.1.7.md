**Polestar Client 0.1.7** stops using your graphics card while you play, tells Polestar when a new version is out, and says why when it can't reach the community site or GitHub.

Already have Polestar Client 0.1.4 or newer? Click **Update to 0.1.7** in the bar at the top of the window, and the app restarts on the new version. On 0.1.2 or older, look for it at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**).

## What's new

- **Tells Polestar about new versions.** After each check for addon updates (and after installing or updating Polestar), Polestar Client writes the newest Polestar release into the addon, so Polestar 0.3.7's update card in the game knows what's out and that the Client can update it for you. The game reads it at your next login or /reload.

## What's fixed

- **No more GPU use in the background.** Polestar Client kept a few of its animations running forever, the twinkling stars in the header and the breathing status light, even while minimized or closed to the tray. On Windows that kept up to 15% of the graphics card busy beside the game. Now the stars and the status light settle within half a minute, and when the window is minimized, in the tray, or left unfocused for 20 seconds, everything holds still until you come back to it. Only work in progress (a spinner, an upload) still moves, and only while you can see it. You no longer need to turn off Windows' **Animation effects** for this. Thanks asymptotic1 for the report and the measurements.
- **Says why it can't connect.** When checking for community guide, addon or client updates, or downloading one, couldn't reach polestar.guide or GitHub, Polestar Client said only "error sending request". Now it names the cause (the address couldn't be looked up, the connection was refused or reset, a certificate it can't check, or no answer in time), and what to try: if the address opens in your browser, a firewall, antivirus, VPN or proxy is probably blocking Polestar Client. A wrong clock on your PC is named, with the time it says.
