**Polestar Client 0.1.3** sends your play again after an upload stopped it, shows its own updates where you can't miss them, and shares your play by default.

Already have Polestar Client? It offers this update by itself: look for **Update to 0.1.3** at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**), click it, and the app restarts on the new version. From this version on, an update shows across the top of the window instead.

## What's fixed

- **Uploads carry on after the game frees space.** Once the Recorder had removed chunks the server already had, the Client stopped with "The Recorder's file can't be read: PolestarRecorderDB.chunks has an unexpected value", and sent nothing more. Fixed: the Client reads those files again and sends the play that waited. Thanks Fyurie for the report.

## What's new

- **Updates you can't miss.** When a new Polestar Client is out, a bar across the top of every page says so, with **Update to <version>**: one click, and the app restarts on it. Settings > About keeps the same button.
- **Sharing is on by default.** Polestar Client now shares your play unless you say otherwise: the quests, places, creatures and vendors the Recorder notes as you play, with no character, realm or account names (like any website, the server sees your IP address). It's how the guides and the Forever database get better for everyone. A notice says so once, with a **Stop sharing** button, and **What we send** and **Activity** show exactly what was sent.
  - Already stopped sharing in the Client, or typed `/psrec off` in the game? You stay off; nothing changes for you.
  - The Recorder on its own, without the Client, still records nothing until you type `/psrec on`.
