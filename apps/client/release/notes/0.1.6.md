**Polestar Client 0.1.6** gets through on PCs where it couldn't reach the Polestar server, says clearly what's going on with the game, and sends what the Recorder 0.3.6 maps in Forever's new zones and dungeons.

Already have Polestar Client 0.1.4? Click **Update to 0.1.6** in the bar at the top of the window, and the app restarts on the new version. On 0.1.2 or older, look for it at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**).

## What's new

- **Explorer mode.** In Forever's new zones and dungeons, Polestar Recorder 0.3.6 logs a little more about the game world while you record there, to map them: creatures seen and where, finer positions of your path there, the quest log's map points, dungeon floors, boss pulls, the area each quest giver stands in and the objects you open. Nothing more about you. Polestar Client 0.1.6 knows these records, so they're sent (older clients leave records they don't know out). **What we send** shows them in plain words, and its **Never sent** tab says what explorer mode adds.
- **Explorer mode switch.** **Settings > Recording > Explorer mode** turns it off (or on again) from your next login or /reload, as `/psrec explore off` does in the game.
- **"Too many new registrations today."** When the server turns away a new install because too many registered from your network today, **Status** now says so, and that your play stays on this PC and is sent tomorrow, instead of "Can't reach the server". Other times the server asks to wait read "The server asked to wait".
- **Gets through where it couldn't.** When it can't connect to the Polestar recording server the normal way, Polestar Client now tries other ways before giving up: IPv4 only; its own list of certificate authorities added to your system's, when your system doesn't know the one that signed the server's certificate (your system still checks the certificate as always); and its own DNS lookup of the Polestar server, by DNS over HTTPS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Google (8.8.8.8), which then see that this PC looked up data.polestar.guide. The first that works is used for the recording server until the next start, and **Status** says which. If your proxy itself can't be reached, that one upload goes without it. Certificates are always checked.
- **Says why.** When it still can't connect, **Status** says why (a DNS failure, a refused connection, a certificate it can't check) and what to try, instead of only "check your internet connection". A wrong clock on your PC is named, with the time it says.
- **Recording on or off.** The game tells Polestar Client whether recording is on only when you log out or type /reload, so **Status** now says "Off in the game as of your last logout or /reload", and to /reload after turning recording on.
- **Deleting your data** says why when nothing was deleted.
- **"World of Warcraft is running" only when it is.** Polestar Client took the game folder's World of Warcraft Launcher for the game, so it blocked addon updates while only Battle.net was open.
- **Safer connections.** Uploads to the Polestar server never follow a redirect and use HTTPS only, and the updater no longer carries an old zip library with known advisories.
