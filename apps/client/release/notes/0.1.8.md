**Polestar Client 0.1.8** removes Polestar 0.4.2's regional walking-map folders on update, Repair and uninstall, so they leave your AddOns folder and the game's AddOns list.

Already have Polestar Client 0.1.4 or newer? Click **Update to 0.1.8** in the bar at the top of the window, and the app restarts on the new version. On 0.1.2 or older, look for it at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**).

## What's fixed

- **No more 127 leftover map folders.** Polestar 0.4.2 installed its walking maps as 127 extra folders named `Polestar_Nav_R…`, and Polestar 0.4.2.1 no longer needs them. Polestar Client now removes them when it updates or repairs Polestar, and when you uninstall it, so they disappear from your AddOns folder and from the AddOns list in the game. If you updated Polestar before updating the Client, click **Repair** on Polestar in the Addons page once to clear them. Only Polestar's own folders go: a folder with that name that you made yourself is left alone, as long as its .toc has a different title.
- **Update all counts only what it can update.** The Update all count and the Addons badge in the sidebar now include only updates Polestar Client can install together. A community guide whose publisher or folders changed still waits for you to confirm it on Community Guides, and Update all no longer offers an update it then can't do. Thanks shacklef00rd and PRDesrochers for the reports (#2182).
- **Failed uploads stay marked as failed.** The Activity page no longer marks a failed upload as sent again just because a different account uploaded later. Retry times now follow the same account. Thanks shacklef00rd for the report (#2185).
- **Updating while the game runs.** With World of Warcraft open, the Addons page now says that addons you install or update load after /reload or the next start, instead of asking you to close the game first.
