**Polestar Client 0.1.1** fixes choosing your game on the first screen.

Already have Polestar Client? It offers this update by itself: look for **Update to 0.1.1** at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**), click it, and the app restarts on the new version.

## What's fixed

- **Picking your game now shows the tick.** On Windows, choosing your game on the first screen ("Find World of Warcraft: Forever") saved it, but no game showed as selected, so it looked as if none could be picked. The same happened on Linux when the game sits behind a linked folder. Thanks Pynte for the report.
- **Only WoW: Forever is offered.** The beta has ended, so a `_classic_beta_` folder left over from it is no longer listed next to your `_forever_` game.
