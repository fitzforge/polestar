**Polestar Client 0.1.5** gets through on PCs where it couldn't reach the Polestar server, and says clearly what's going on with the game.

Already have Polestar Client 0.1.4? Click **Update to 0.1.5** in the bar at the top of the window, and the app restarts on the new version. On 0.1.2 or older, look for it at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**).

## What's new

- **Gets through where it couldn't.** When the normal connection fails, Polestar Client now tries other ways before giving up: IPv4 only, its own list of certificate authorities beside your system's, its own DNS lookup (DNS over HTTPS), and without your proxy. The first that works is used until the next start, and **Status** says which. Certificates are always checked.
- **Says why.** When it still can't connect, **Status** says why (a DNS failure, a refused connection, a certificate it can't check) and what to try, instead of only "check your internet connection". A wrong clock on your PC is named, with the time it says.
- **Recording on or off.** The game tells Polestar Client whether recording is on only when you log out or type /reload, so **Status** now says "Off in the game as of your last logout or /reload", and to /reload after turning recording on.
- **Deleting your data** says why when nothing was deleted.
