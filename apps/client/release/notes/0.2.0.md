**Polestar Client 0.2.0** is in your language.

Already have Polestar Client? It offers this update by itself: look for **Update to 0.2.0** at the bottom of the sidebar (or in **Settings > About**), click it, and the app restarts on the new version.

## Polestar Client in your language

Polestar Client now speaks the same languages as the Polestar addon:

- Deutsch: German
- Español (España): Spanish, Spain
- Español (Latinoamérica): Spanish, Latin America
- Français: French
- Português (Brasil): Brazilian Portuguese
- Русский: Russian
- 한국어: Korean
- 简体中文: Chinese, simplified
- 繁體中文: Chinese, traditional

It follows your computer's language by itself. To pick another one, open **Settings** and choose it under **Language** (Automatic goes back to your computer's). The tray menu and the notifications follow it too.

The translations are machine-made for now. If something reads wrong in your language, tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/polestar): native speakers' corrections are very welcome.

## What's fixed

- **Your characters' names stay private in Chinese, Japanese and Korean text too.** Before sending, Polestar Client takes your characters' names out of quest and gossip text. In languages written without spaces, a name that runs straight into the next word (such as a Korean name followed by "가") could be missed. Now it is taken out there as well.

**First launch:**
- **Windows** may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** the app is signed and checked by Apple; open the .dmg, drag Polestar Client into Applications, and open it.
