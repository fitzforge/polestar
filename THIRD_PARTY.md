## Distributing Polestar

Polestar's own code and guides are GPL-3.0 (see LICENSE). What a release
(`node tools/polestar.js pack`) holds from the inputs above:

- QuestieDB: facts only, credited in the table above, in the compiled guide
  pack, `Polestar_QuestDB` and `Polestar_Codex`. QuestieDB publishes no
  licence; if its maintainers ask, they come out: `data build --no-questie`
  builds without them, after which `Polestar_QuestDB` and `Polestar_Codex` are
  rebuilt and the guides that need
  Forever-only facts give them themselves or are recompiled from other
  sources; the entries of `tools/lib/codex/entrances.json` that cite it are
  removed (the Codex falls back to recordings, guides and corpse points).
- DungeonJournal: nothing. The compiler and a release `codex build` do not
  read `data/forever/dungeons.json`; a quest only it knows gets its title and
  level in the guide, from a public source the guide names.
- The Codex (`Polestar_Codex`): always, built by `codex build` from
  release-safe inputs only: the Forever and Era client tables, pfQuest (MIT),
  QuestieDB facts (as above), ForeverLevelingBiS (MIT), Wowhead Forever's
  per-entity facts (the cache under `data/sources/wowhead/codex/`), the web
  facts in `data/forever` (XP, Forever change notes, corrections, each with
  its source),
  the cited reputation gates of `tools/lib/codex/repgates.json`, our own
  recordings and our own guides. Its `LICENSE-DATA.txt` lists every
  source with its version and licence. AtlasLoot (GPL-2.0, which GPL-3.0-only
  Polestar cannot take in), RareScanner (All Rights Reserved) and
  DungeonJournal (no licence) feed only `codex build --local-extras`, a
  comparison build for the developer's machine whose meta says so; `pack`
  refuses it, and any Codex whose sources name them.
- Carbonite's zone crossings: always, in `TravelData.lua`, under GPL-3.0.
  They are data of a GPL program, not Polestar's reference data: the
  guide-pack exception does not cover them and the compiler never puts them
  in a pack.
- The local zone crossings pack (`Polestar_Travel`) never ships: its
  crossings come from a local file (an import of another addon's data, for
  the developer's machine only).
- Recast's `dividePoly` (zlib) and TrinityCore's `GetLiquidTypeId`
  (GPL-2.0-or-later, compatible with Polestar's GPL-3.0) are ported into the
  navigation grid's tool code, which a release does not hold.
- Polestar's walking router (started from Shortest Path Forever's, his
  copyright kept) and Polestar's maps of Eastern Kingdoms, Kalimdor and
  Zephras Isle, grown from his format (`Polestar_Nav`): always, under
  GPL-3.0-or-later with his copyright and `CREDITS.md`. They are code
  and data of a GPL program, not reference data: the guide-pack exception
  does not cover them and the compiler never puts them in a pack.
- Ding! and DingDB: anything taken from them may ship, under GPL-3.0 with
  Yendi's credit and, for DingDB data, Ding's `Ding/DB/CREDITS.md`. His
  permission covers the whole project and its later versions. The names
  DingDB takes from QuestieDB follow the QuestieDB rule above: if Questie's
  maintainers ask, they come out: `i18n names --no-questie` rebuilds the
  names addons from pfQuest alone.
- The names addons (`Polestar_Names_<locale>`): always, the game's names in
  each language from DingDB (above) and pfQuest (MIT), only for the quests,
  NPCs, objects and items Polestar's guides, quest database and Codex name.
