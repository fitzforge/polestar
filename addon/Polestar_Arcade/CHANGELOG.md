# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Renamed from WoWDoom to **Freedoom Arcade**.
  - The addon folders are now `FreedoomArcade` and `FreedoomArcade_E1`–`E4`.
  - `/arcade` and `/freedoom` open the game; `/doom` still works.
- The release zip now contains only the addon folders, ready for the CurseForge app.
- The GPL license is included in the addon folder.
- Releases are built, tested and published automatically from GitHub.

## 0.2.2

- Right-click the minimap button for a save/load menu:
  - open or close the game
  - quick save and quick load
  - five named save slots, each showing its map and time
- `/doom save 1-5` and `/doom load 1-5`.

## 0.2.1

- Minimap button with the marine's face:
  - left-click opens or closes the game
  - drag to move it around the minimap
  - `/doom minimap` hides it
- Also listed in the addon compartment.

## 0.2.0

- Quick save and quick load with F6 / F9. Saves the full level state and persists across reloads.
- Pause on aggro: entering combat auto-saves, pauses and closes the game so your keyboard goes back to your character.

## 0.1.1

- Automap on Tab or M, with zoom on +/- or the mouse wheel.
- Map colors:
  - walls red
  - floor steps brown
  - ceiling changes yellow
  - locked doors in their key's color

## 0.1.0

- First release: all 36 Freedoom Phase 1 maps playable in a window inside the game.
